A different energy question

Better tools.
More possibilities.

Lux asks how AI might help us develop more effective energy systems, with fusion as its first focus.

Fusion joins light atomic nuclei and releases energy. Turning that process into useful electricity also requires dependable control, resilient materials, a working fuel cycle, and a whole plant that performs together. [L08]

AI offers tools for learning from experiments, forecasting behavior, and exploring designs. Lux brings that research into view, keeping demonstrated results and future ambitions distinct. [L06]

↔

Our companion project Spira explores AI’s electricity and water demands. Lux explores AI’s potential contribution to energy innovation. Elsewhere, Dux considers AI and education, while Valens offers a written support resource for people concerned that chatbot conversations may be affecting their sense of reality.

Keep the whole system in view

A better plasma is part of
a much bigger picture.

Useful electricity depends on more than plasma performance. Heating, cooling, magnets, fuel handling, and other plant systems also consume power. Fusion gain and net electricity are different measures. [L07]

Lux’s ambition is to explore AI’s contribution to more effective energy systems. The studies here do not establish a measured energy saving for Lux or an operating AI-controlled fusion power plant.
