The first public library

Project Lux’s first site release brings together 282 dated research notes, eight featured reference briefs, and an introduction to AI’s role in fusion research.

The site follows the shared Voelori style while using a distinct light-and-fusion visual identity. This is a publication of existing research records, not a claim that every source was independently revalidated at launch.
