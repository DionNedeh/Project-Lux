Tokamak Energy's pre-concept low-aspect-ratio power plant, successor to ST40, and a DOE milestone-programme design. An 11-paper Focus Collection headed by Willis et al. 2026.

Its organising idea is financial. Phased operation — pilot, upgrade, commercial — with permanent assets (magnet cage, vessel, cryostat, civil works) built to commercial spec from the outset and in-vessel systems replaceable every 5 full-power years. The first design in Document 30 organised commercially rather than physically.

Warning

The source writes Q unqualified Table 1 gives "Q (–)" with no subscript; 1500/75 = 20 makes it Q_sci — this vault's derivation, not the source's label (Q — the Four Gain Definitions).

What distinguishes it#

  • Breeds on pure, unenriched lithium — liquid, helium-cooled, integral extraction loop. The only such route here (Lithium-6), and now evidenced: 086004 selects natural lithium.
  • V-4Cr-4Ti, not RAFM steel, for the centre-column shield box — though F82H and 316L(N)-IG appear elsewhere.
  • Shielding, not breeding, drives the design, modelled in the in-house Geant4 code G4Tokamak (Simulation, Codes and Nuclear Data).
  • Not pulsed: a 5-minute ramp capping grid draw at 500 MW needs 1–2 MWh storage.

Warning

CORRECTION 2026-11-02 — the "> 30 FPY" above is an intent, not a result 086003 gives peak HTS fast flux 9.5 × 10⁹ against its own < 4 × 10⁹ limit — 2.4× over, for an estimated 13 fpy. A 2400 MW variant at A = 2.31 recovers ~25 fpy. See Superconductors and Magnet Engineering.

Fuel cycle#

1.58 kg start-up inventory, 152-day doubling time, 1.39 kg holdup, 2.6–2.9% burn fraction, DIR 0.7, no trapping physics (Tritium Retention). Full table: Document 28 §6.

Recorded disagreements#

TBR — RESOLVED: three figures, one sensitivity table (Declared TBR Values and Their Conflicts). A new tension replaces it: 086003 gives 1.169–1.194, above 086004's 1.146 baseline. Availability — 37.5% / > 80% / 100% across three sources (Availability and RAMI). Net electric — resolves as phasing, with a residual gap to the 2023 figure.

Evidence limits#

Nothing is built or measured. Eight of ten companion papers remain uncaptured, and nobody can account for tritium in a liquid-lithium breeder (Tritium Accountancy).
