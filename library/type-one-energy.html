companies

Knoxville, Tennessee. Optimised Stellarator developer, and one of the sector's most transparent.

Infinity Two: a 350 MW plant at a Tennessee Valley Authority former coal site, targeted mid-2030s.

Why it is credible#

Published a complete physics basis in the Journal of Plasma Physics, including breeder blanket and tritium fuel-cycle feasibility — a level of public design detail rare for a private company, and one of the clearest signals separating serious efforts from pitch decks.

6 August 2026: licensed four cryogenic pellet fuelling inventions from Oak Ridge National Laboratory, including an extrusion system producing pellets at reactor-relevant rates.

A US Department of Energy — Office of Fusion Milestone Program awardee. Total funding past $160 m including an $87 m convertible note.
