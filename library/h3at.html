Hydrogen-3 Advanced Technology — a UK Atomic Energy Authority facility developed with Eni for tritium processing and handling at plant scale.

Its purpose is to develop the skills and technologies for processing and handling tritium within future fusion power plants — the practical counterpart to LIBRTI's breeding work.

An oil major funding tritium infrastructure is itself a notable signal about how energy incumbents are positioning. See Vacuum, Wall Conditioning and the Tritium Plant for what a tritium plant actually contains.
