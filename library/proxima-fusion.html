companies

Munich, spun out of Max Planck Institute for Plasma Physics — the Wendelstein 7-X lineage.

$752 m declared; $518 m raised in the year to July 2026 — the second-largest raise in the industry that year, after Commonwealth Fusion Systems.

Published Stellaris: the first fully integrated quasi-isodynamic Stellarator power plant design optimised simultaneously for physics and engineering feasibility — which is precisely the gap between W7-X and a plant. See Quasi-symmetry and Quasi-isodynamicity.

Part of a notably strong German cluster alongside Marvel Fusion, Focused Energy and Gauss Fusion, supported by a €2 bn federal Fusion Action Plan and three new fusion hubs (VEGA, STRIDE, MAT-TRIX).
