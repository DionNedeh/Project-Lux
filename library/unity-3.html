facilitiesmaterials

The first facility generating 14 MeV neutrons to evaluate commercial breeding blanket designs and validate tritium production.

Built at Oak Ridge National Laboratory with Kyoto Fusioneering, which relocated its US headquarters to Oak Ridge on 11 August 2026 to do it. Complements UNITY-1 (Japan) and UNITY-2 (Canada, under development).

Why it matters more than its budget suggests#

Tritium Breeding Ratio (TBR) is the field's second-hardest problem and has never been demonstrated. ITER's Test Blanket Modules are the first in-device test — from 2039.

And per Nuclear Data and FENDL, a blanket design validated only computationally has not been validated: cross-section uncertainty is a meaningful fraction of the entire 10% design margin between TBR = 1.1 and TBR = 1.0.

UNITY-3, alongside the UK's LIBRTI, is where that question gets answered this decade rather than next.
