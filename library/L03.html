What this reference supports

The Lux source note describes a neural forecaster trained on DIII-D and applied to KSTAR without retraining its weights, after physics-consistent preprocessing. The task is forecasting the onset of Type-I edge-localized modes.

Where the evidence stops

One source–target pair is not universal portability. Forecasting is not closed-loop suppression of an instability. Detailed findings retain the source note’s medium-confidence designation.

Source & provenance

Joung, S. et al. Explainable tokamak-agnostic ELM forecasting. Communications Physics (2026). Bibliographic identity and title as recorded in the Lux note.
