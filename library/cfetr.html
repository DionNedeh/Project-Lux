machines

The China Fusion Engineering Test Reactor — explicitly designed to bridge the gap between ITER and DEMO, a step the Western programmes do not have.

Phase I targets — precise and modest#
Fusion power 50–200 MW
Q_plasma 1–5
Tritium Breeding Ratio (TBR) > 1.0
Neutron irradiation ~10 dpa

Two things stand out. The 10 dpa target means CFETR is designed partly as a materials irradiation facility, addressing in-machine what IFMIF-DONES and Fusion Prototypic Neutron Source (FPNS) address ex-machine. And TBR > 1.0 as a Phase I requirement means China intends to demonstrate tritium self-sufficiency in an operating machine rather than in test modules.

Phase II scales toward gigawatt-class fusion power. Assessed with the PROCESS and GASC systems codes.
