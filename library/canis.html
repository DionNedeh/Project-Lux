Thea Energy's 3 × 3 planar REBCO High-Temperature Superconductor shaping-coil test array, built to test the magnet-manufacturing and field-control approach intended for Eos. It is a magnet prototype, not a plasma device.

The programme manufactured nine coils from 45 double-pancake windings, with conductor from three suppliers, and operated the integrated array at 20 K. Two static field patterns measured 25 cm from the array matched predictions with RMS errors of 0.56% and 0.60%, inside the programme's ±1% target. The winding line reached roughly half a day takt time and no more than three days total production time per double pancake.

The boundary matters. Canis was flat, whereas a reactor's shaping coils would conform to a three-dimensional surface. It generated no net toroidal flux, closed flux surfaces, quasisymmetry or plasma. Transient control, several quench cases, robustness to manufacturing defects and dense field-shaping-unit integration remained planned work. Prototyping and Test of the Canis HTS Planar Coil Array for Stellarator Field Shaping therefore establishes a component-level proof of concept, not an integrated stellarator result.

The gap it has to cross is now specified: Helios's design calls for 324 shaping coils plus 12 plasma-encircling coils at up to 20 T on-coil (Overview of the Helios Design: A Practical Planar Coil Stellarator Fusion Power Plant). Canis was nine coils, flat, at 20 K.
