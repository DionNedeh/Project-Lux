The isotope on which the entire D-T fuel cycle depends, via n + ⁶Li → T + ⁴He + 4.8 MeV.

The bottleneck#

Natural lithium is 7.5% ⁶Li. Breeding blankets need 30–90% enrichment.

Warning

Not every blanket does — and that changes the chokepoint argument Added 2026-10-19. ST-E1 breeds on pure, unenriched lithium. Willis et al. 2026 select it deliberately, to reach a high volumetric TBR "without reliance on Li-6 enrichment" and without extensive use of toxic multipliers such as beryllium, while keeping radioactive waste acceptable.

The 30–90% figure above holds for the designs it describes, which are the majority. What changes is the inference: enrichment is a property of particular blanket architectures, not of D-T fusion. A low-aspect-ratio device with thick, high-coverage blanket volume can trade geometry for enrichment — so the exposure below threatens most current designs, not the fuel cycle.

Evidenced 2026-11-02, superseding this page's earlier record that the claim was asserted and an attempt to evidence it had failed. 086004, read in full, selects natural liquid lithium outright. No breeding blanket of any kind has operated in a device.

Historic enrichment capacity is concentrated in Russia and China — a sovereignty exposure flagged by the DOE roadmap and industry analysis, alongside REBCO High-Temperature Superconductor tape, tungsten and helium (Critical Materials and Supply Chains).

A second-order cost: life-cycle assessment finds fusion's water usage higher than comparable technologies, driven by mining and refining critical minerals (Safety, Environment and Decommissioning).

The UK's LIBRTI (£220m, 2025/26–2029/30) is the first facility purpose-built for lithium breeding technology at scale.

Note

Enrichment is necessary but not sufficient Solving ⁶Li enrichment does not solve breeding. Abdou et al. 2021 put the maximum achievable TBR at 1.15 even with detailed blanket designs, and showed the requirement is set by plasma burn fraction and fuel processing time rather than by breeder composition. A plant with abundant enriched lithium and a 0.36% burn fraction still fails. Enrichment is one of several necessary conditions, and not the binding one.

Note

One architecture routes around enrichment — evidenced 2026-11-02 UKAEA states that V-4Cr-4Ti's properties "reduce the need for neutron multipliers or lithium-6 enrichment in liquid lithium breeder blanket systems", and ST-E1 selects natural lithium outright. The Russia/China concentration therefore binds a class of blanket designs, not D-T fusion — at the cost of the vanadium supply chain.
