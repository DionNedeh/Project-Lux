The industry survey of record, and the primary source for all company-level funding, headcount, approach and timeline data in this wiki. Sixth annual edition.

Headline figures#
Total funding to date $14,242,143,000
Raised in 12 months to July 2026 $4,476,143,000 (+69%)
Private / public $13.26 bn / $0.98 bn
Companies surveyed 56 (23 in 2021)
Direct employees 5,606 · supply chain 10,497
Further capital each company needs avg $2.7 bn, median $1.2 bn

Billion-dollar companies, all US: Commonwealth Fusion Systems, Helion Energy, TAE Technologies, SHINE Technologies, Pacific Fusion.

Approach split (56 companies)#

Magnetic confinement 26 · inertial 12 · magneto-inertial 9 · hybrid electrostatic 7 · muon-catalysed 1. Stellarators (8) now outnumber tokamaks (5). Fuel: D-T 40, p-¹¹B 5, D-D 5, D-³He 2.

The challenge ranking (near-term / long-term)#

Funding 30/18 · power efficiency and high Q 29/23 · neutron-resilient materials 29/25 · tritium self-sufficiency 25/23 · integrated systems engineering 19/10 · plasma science 15/7 · supply chain readiness 15/20.

The industry ranks plasma science seventh. Materials and tritium sit at the top and stay there.

Stated caveat#

The FIA states plainly that its Chinese coverage is incomplete — several companies with reported government and private funding declined to respond. Any national comparison drawn from this data understates Chinese activity.
