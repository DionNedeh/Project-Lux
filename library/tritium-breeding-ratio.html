Tritium bred per tritium consumed. The precondition for fusion existing as an energy source.

Why it is not optional#

A 1 GW plant burns ~55.6 kg/year against a world civilian inventory of 30–40 kg, produced as a CANDU byproduct at under 0.13 kg per reactor-year, at $30,000–35,000/gram, decaying 5.5% annually. One plant would consume more than the world holds in its first year, and there is no market to buy from.

The reactions#

n + ⁶Li → T + ⁴He + 4.8 MeV      (exothermic, large low-energy cross-section)
n + ⁷Li → T + ⁴He + n − 2.5 MeV  (endothermic, regenerates the neutron)

Each fusion yields one neutron, and neutrons are lost to structure, ports and diagnostics — so TBR > 1 generally needs neutron multiplication by Beryllium or lead. Design target: TBR > 1.1 (DOE roadmap); failure threshold 1.0. A 10% margin.

The achievability bands#

From Abdou et al. 2021, the primary source for a band the vault previously carried on the DOE roadmap's authority alone:

Required TBR Self-sufficiency
> 1.15 Unlikely
1.05 – 1.15 Possible
≤ 1.05 Attained with high confidence

Maximum achievable for the most detailed blanket designs is estimated at 1.15, with high confidence only at 1.05 — so requirement and capability occupy the same interval. That is the whole difficulty.

Warning

The uncertainty is comparable to the margin, and biased Nuclear data uncertainty runs to a few percent, with 12% flux discrepancies between cross-section libraries (Simulation, Codes and Nuclear Data). Worse, it is biased: integral experiments found calculation overestimated tritium production by an average factor of 1.14 — an error in the dangerous direction, larger than the margin it eats. Abdou et al. call it "alarming" and report a recommended safety factor of >1.1 to 1.2.

What actually binds#

Not this quantity. Breeding is downstream of the plasma: what binds is η_f · f_b with processing time, while Availability and RAMI sets a floor below which no blanket suffices. No blanket improvement rescues a plant whose burn fraction is too low (Engineering Challenges). The surplus is also the only divertible tritium a plant makes — Tritium Accountancy.

Declared values, and their conflicts#

Design TBR
DOE roadmap target > 1.1
Helios 1.3 idealised; < 1.15 required
ST-E1 1.146 full coverage, 1.085–1.14 with penetrations — reconciled 2026-11-02
ST-E1 neutronics 1.169 (MCNP) · 1.194 (G4Tokamak) — above its own fuel-cycle baseline
ARC- / STEP-class required < 1.2, if burn efficiency reaches 0.5–1%

Every declared figure, the disagreements between and within them, and what could not be resolved: Declared TBR Values and Their Conflicts.

Warning

The required value moves too, and upward Declared values are what designs claim to achieve. What they must achieve is a separate quantity, and Meschini et al. 2025 raise it 2–5% once trapping is modelled. Against an achievable ceiling Abdou puts at 1.15, a requirement already near 1.1 has little room to absorb that.

Never demonstrated. ITER's Test Blanket Modules are first, from 2039; UNITY-3 and LIBRTI answer it sooner.
