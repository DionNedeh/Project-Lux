The most rigorous independent assessment of fusion economics available. A stochastic Monte Carlo harmonisation of 56 references across magnetic, inertial and magneto-inertial confinement.

Results, mature technology#

Line Mean LCOE
Magnetic confinement $114.6/MWh
Inertial confinement $110.3/MWh
Magneto-inertial $143.9/MWh

The conclusion, quoted#

"We find that these projected values are rather optimistic when compared to other literature or comparable technologies like fission. We therefore urge policymakers to caution when potential fusion developers refer to the potential economic competitiveness of fusion power plants."

Highlights from the paper#

  • Analysis of fusion cost and technology data from 56 references
  • LCOE determined to be lower than today's fission reactors
  • Analysis of assumed learning rates shows very optimistic assumptions — reaching these figures implies learning rates above 30%. Solar PV achieved 20–25%; Western fission achieved negative learning
  • Technological maturity still low; initial actual cost expected to be much higher

This is the counterweight to every "too cheap to meter" claim in the sector. See Fusion LCOE and Capital Cost and The Discourse.
