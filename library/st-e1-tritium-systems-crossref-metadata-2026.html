RESOLVED — the three conflicting TBR figures were one table all along#

The vault recorded three irreconcilable ST-E1 breeding values. Table 2 is a sensitivity table, and two of the three are rows in it:

Case TBR Δ
Baseline, full breeding coverage 1.146 0.0%
33% Limiters 1.142 −0.4%
33% ECRH 1.135 −0.9%
33% ECRH + 33% Limiters 1.135 −1.0%
33% ICRH 1.131 −1.3%
100% ECRH 1.122 −2.1%
100% Pipes + Stiffening Plates 1.113 −2.8%
100% Limiters 1.107 −3.4%
100% ICRH 1.085 −8.2%

Warning

CORRECTION 2026-11-02 — Yang's 1.113 was never an independent third estimate Yang 2026's modelled 1.113 is exactly this table's "100% Pipes + Stiffening Plates" row. The overview's ">1.14" is the full-coverage 1.146; its prose "~1.1" is the post-insertion range. One quantity, three penetration assumptions — not three disagreeing measurements. The 1.20 is an "initial design target … for rough scoping studies", never a prediction. See Declared TBR Values and Their Conflicts.

EVIDENCED — the unenriched lithium claim#

"Only solid breeders and natural lithium achieve the target TBR in excess of 1.20." The paper selects natural, unenriched liquid lithium. The claim this vault carried as asserted since 19 October is now evidenced from the design paper itself, and independently in outline by UKAEA via Vanadium Alloys. See Lithium-6.

The fuel cycle, quantified#

Quantity Value
Start-up inventory 1.58 kg (incl. 20% commissioning contingency); 1.31 kg tritium-lean
Doubling time 152 days
Operational inventory I_op 2.4 kg, reached in 85 days
Total inventory excl. storage 1.39 kg at TBE 2%; 2.42 kg at TBE 1%
Burn fraction 2.6% at η_fuel 76% (R = 0.1); 2.9% at 68% (R = 0.2)
DIR fraction 0.7, protium 1 at% in / 0.8 at% out
Blanket inventory 0.31 kg, in a 360,000 kg loop flowing 296 kg s⁻¹

Extraction is a concentration-dependent TES: 1% efficient below an onset concentration (100 ppb worked example), rising to 80% above ~1 ppm, with a 20% bleed and per-segment lithium loops. Lithium tritide electrolysis is "a potential method"; no final selection appears in the text retrieved. Full fuel-cycle table: Document 28 §6.

Warning

NEW CONFLICT — availability, now with a third figure This paper assumes 100% availability, noting it as a simplification offset by a 20% contingency. The design mission targets > 80%; Yang 2026 models 37.5%. The design paper's own fuel-cycle analysis uses the most optimistic of the three. See Availability and RAMI.

What it does not establish#

  • Table 1's breeder-to-value mapping could not be determined from the retrieved text: five breeders (Li₁₇Pb₈₃, Li, FLiBe, Li₄SiO₄, Li₂TiO₃) against TBR cells 1.04, 1.26, 0.92, 1.27, one cell missing. Which breeders clear 1.20 is therefore not recorded here.
  • No trapping physics. First wall and divertor are assigned 0.012–0.024 kg; see Tritium Retention for why that is not comparable with trapping-inclusive models.
  • Nothing measured. No liquid-lithium breeder blanket has operated in any fusion device.
