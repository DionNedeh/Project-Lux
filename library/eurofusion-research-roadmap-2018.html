policyprograms

The document that has governed European fusion since 2012, structured around eight missions.

Targets: all know-how in place to start EU DEMO construction in the 2030s, and fusion electricity on the European grid by 2050.

The European strategy it encodes is deliberately conservative: let ITER retire the physics risk, then build a machine that requires no new physics.

Being revised#

EUROfusion states the programme "will move from being laboratory-based and science-driven towards an industry- and technology-driven venture." The EU's first-ever Fusion Strategy is due in 2026, alongside a public-private partnership with the EU fusion industry and alignment to ITER Baseline 2024.
