facilitiesmaterials

The International Fusion Materials Irradiation Facility – DEMO-Oriented NEutron Source, at Granada. Europe's answer to fusion's hardest problem.

A 40 MeV, 125 mA deuteron beam strikes a flowing lithium jet, producing neutrons by Li(d,xn) stripping with a spectrum approximating D-T.

Phase Target dose
Phase 1 ~20 dpa
Phase 2 ~50 dpa

Under construction. Not operating.

Why it is on the critical path#

A power plant first wall sees ~15 dpa per year; ITER will accumulate under 2 dpa over its entire life. And per Helium Embrittlement, fission irradiation data cannot substitute, because the helium-per-dpa ratio is an order of magnitude different.

There is no operating fusion-spectrum neutron source anywhere in the world. Every pilot plant planned for the 2030s will be built on extrapolated and surrogate data.
