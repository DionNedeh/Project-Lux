Wellington, New Zealand. Levitated dipole — a superconducting ring levitated inside the vacuum chamber, mimicking a planetary magnetosphere. Remarkable stability and natural density peaking.

Achieved plasma in 2024, having been founded in 2021.

OpenStar is the clearest evidence that fusion's entry cost has fallen enough for a small-nation startup to produce a real result in a configuration nobody had commercialised. See Fusion's Global Periphery.
