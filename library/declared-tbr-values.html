What designs claim for TBR, and where those claims disagree — with each other and, in two cases, internally.

Judged against Abdou's bands: unlikely above 1.15, possible 1.05–1.15, maximum achievable ~1.15.

Helios — 1.3 and <1.15, in one paper#

Helios quotes an idealised TBR of 1.3 from a PbLi blanket enriched to 65% Lithium-6, while its own sensitivity analysis concludes TBR under 1.15 suffices (Overview of the Helios Design: A Practical Planar Coil Stellarator Fusion Power Plant).

Note

A conflict of kind, not of fact The 1.3 sits outside Abdou's achievable range; the <1.15 agrees with him almost exactly. But 1.3 is a neutronics figure of merit and 1.15 a system requirement net of ports, penetrations and diagnostics. Helios's two numbers bracket Abdou's boundary rather than contradicting it. Both stand.

ST-E1 — RESOLVED 2026-11-02#

Three figures stood recorded as irreconcilable. Read in full, 086004 shows they are one quantity at three penetration assumptions — rows of, or ranges from, its Table 2 sensitivity study:

Figure What it actually is
1.146 Table 2 baseline, full breeding coverage — the overview's "> 1.14"
1.113 Table 2's 100% Pipes + Stiffening Plates row (−2.8%), and exactly Yang's model input
1.085 Table 2's 100% ICRH row (−8.2%), floor of the quoted 1.085–1.14 range
~1.1 The overview's §3.2 prose — that same post-insertion range
1.20 An "initial design target … for rough scoping studies", never a prediction

Note

CORRECTION 2026-11-02 — Yang's 1.113 was not a third estimate This page previously recorded 1.113 as an independent model input disagreeing with the overview. It is a row of ST-E1's own sensitivity table. That conflict was a provenance artefact and is now closed.

A genuine tension remains. 086003's neutronics gives 1.169 (MCNP) and 1.194 (G4Tokamak) — both above 086004's full-coverage 1.146 and above Abdou's achievable ceiling. Same plant, same Focus Collection, different codes and geometry revisions. No source reconciles the two papers.

ST-E1 breeds without ⁶Li enrichment, and that is now evidenced. 086004 selects natural liquid lithium; UKAEA states independently that V-4Cr-4Ti reduces the need for enrichment or beryllium multiplication in liquid-lithium blankets. Previously asserted — see Lithium-6.

Why the arithmetic may matter less than it looks#

ST-E1's own fuel-cycle team reports extraction sensitivity and fuelling efficiency as the dominant drivers of inventory performance, rather than marginal improvements in breeding ratio (Tritium Production and Processing Systems for ST-E1) — Abdou's reframing reached independently by a company with every incentive to foreground its breeding instead.

So the gap between 1.1 and 1.14 matters less than that between a 0.36% and a 2% burn fraction — and the ST-E1 spread proved to be exactly that, a provenance defect rather than a design risk.

Warning

What would move these numbers is in none of them Meschini et al. 2025 raise required TBR by 2–5% once trapping is modelled — and no figure on this page includes it.
