The electricity a fusion plant consumes to run itself, subtracted from gross output. The reason Q_sci and Q_eng diverge so sharply.

The load list#

  • Cryoplant for the magnets. Removing heat at 4.5 K costs 250–1,000 W of room-temperature power per watt removed
  • Plasma heating and current drive — tens to >100 MW at 30–50% wall-plug efficiency. Usually the dominant term
  • Coolant pumping — severe for helium, and for liquid metals against MHD drag
  • Tritium plant, vacuum systems, controls

For a tokamak, plausible recirculating fractions are 20–40% of gross output.

The consequence#

Q_eng > 1 typically requires Q_sci in the 10–30+ range. This is why scientific breakeven at Q_sci = 1, while a genuine milestone, is a long way from a generator — and why Q — the Four Gain Definitions insists on saying which Q.

It is also a direct argument for HTS at 20 K rather than LTS at 4.5 K, which cuts the Carnot penalty by roughly 4–5×, and for Direct Energy Conversion, which removes the thermal cycle altogether.

The stellarator claim, quantified#

The stellarator case has always rested partly on low recirculating power: no driven plasma current, no current-drive load, inherently steady-state. Helios puts numbers to it (Overview of the Helios Design: A Practical Planar Coil Stellarator Fusion Power Plant) — 69.8 MW of 459.9 MWe generated, roughly 15%, against the 20–40% above. The dominant tokamak term is simply absent: once ignited it needs under 1 MW of heating, against tens to >100 MW of current drive.

Its Sankey diagram itemises the self-consumption, which is rarer in published designs than it should be:

Load Value
Generated electric 459.9 MW
Recirculating pumps 21.8 MW
Gross cycle electric 438.1 MW
Maintenance and operations 20.0 MW
Tritium plant 15.0 MW
Cryoplant 10.5 MW
ECRH 2.5 MW
Net plant electric 390.1 MW

The four auxiliary loads total exactly the 48 MW the text quotes, so ECRH sits inside that figure rather than beside it. Note what the itemisation shows: no single dominant term. In a tokamak, current drive alone would exceed this entire list.

Warning

A design projection Helios is preconceptual; every figure above is modelled. The values come from the Sankey image, not the text layer of the paper.
