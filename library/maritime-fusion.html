companiesmarkets

San Francisco. Founded by SpaceX and Tesla engineers; Y Combinator W25.

Developing a complete ship-based propulsion system — tokamak, shielding, cryogenic plant, magnet power supplies, tritium handling, heat conversion — on the argument that the whole assembly is far smaller than the fuel tanks required for an ammonia- or hydrogen-powered vessel.

That is the key economic claim: on a ship, volume is revenue. A compact energy source that displaces less cargo commands a premium a land-based plant cannot.

First deployment targeted 2032.

Ships carry roughly 90% of world trade and burn among the dirtiest fuels in use, with no good decarbonisation option — batteries lack the energy density, ammonia and hydrogen displace cargo, biofuels do not scale.
