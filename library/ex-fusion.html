companiesicf

Osaka-based company describing itself as Japan’s first full-stack laser-fusion developer. Its programme spans high-power, high-repetition laser drivers, beam control and target injection—the three coupled systems that distinguish an energy plant from a single-shot physics experiment.

The The Global Fusion Industry Report 2026 records $38 million declared funding, 41 employees, deuterium–tritium fuel and a liquid-metal heat-capture system. In June 2025 the company reported a 10 Hz continuous target-supply and multi-beam irradiation demonstration at its research facility. It also opened a Kyoto R&D facility for laser-fusion applications in 2025.

EX-Fusion announces a system-level demonstration of core plant components in 2030, net electricity in the 2030s and a gigawatt-class reactor by 2045. These are projections. The 10 Hz demonstration addresses repetition and injection, but does not demonstrate target gain, Q_eng, component lifetime or an integrated power cycle.
