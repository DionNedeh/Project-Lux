What this reference supports

FusionMAE uses masked reconstruction of diagnostic signals to learn representations for downstream tasks. The repository describes offline evaluation on recorded HL-3 data, including missing-signal recovery and state-related tasks.

Where the evidence stops

Offline performance does not establish safe closed-loop control, transfer to a different tokamak, or power-plant reliability. The note assigns medium confidence to detailed findings.

Source & provenance

Yang, Z. et al. FusionMAE: self-supervised pretraining for fusion diagnostics and control. Communications Physics 9, 232 (2026). Bibliographic identity and title as recorded in the Lux note.
