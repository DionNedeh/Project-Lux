Remote Applications in Challenging Environments — UK Atomic Energy Authority's robotics and remote-handling centre at Culham Science Centre.

The institutional home of the only operational experience anywhere in maintaining a tritium-contaminated tokamak. See JET Remote Handling and MASCOT.

Recent upgrades to JET — Joint European Torus's Remote Handling Control Room have made the system, in UKAEA's description, one of the most advanced robotics and remote handling systems in the world — and they were made specifically to support the JET Decommissioning and Repurposing programme. The system built to maintain the machine is now being used to take it apart.

RACE's remit is deliberately broader than fusion: it shares problems with nuclear decommissioning (Sellafield, Fukushima), space robotics, subsea intervention and surgical robotics.
