No licensing authority over fusion, but the central coordinating institution.

What it actually does#

  • FENDL — the Fusion Evaluated Nuclear Data Library, on which every neutronics calculation in the field rests
  • World Fusion Outlook — launched annually by Director General Rafael Mariano Grossi. The 2025 edition included a special focus on high-temperature superconducting magnets
  • Fusion Energy Conference (FEC) — biennial. FEC 2025 was the 30th, held in Chengdu, China, with participation from over 50 countries
  • World Fusion Energy Group — second Ministerial Meeting alongside FEC 2025
  • Technical Meeting on Experience in Codes and Standards for Fusion Technology (November 2025) — the main forum for the standards gap. See Standards, Codes and Liability
  • Small and medium device network — a coordinated research project since 2004 spanning 19 countries; a recent five-year CRP linked 24 institutions in 18 countries
  • Technical Meeting on Developing the Fusion Workforce, 27–30 April 2026

IAEA framing, 2025: fusion is "entering a new phase of implementation as it becomes a cornerstone of national energy strategies and industrial planning."

In a field fragmenting into national industrial strategies, the IAEA remains the venue where nuclear data, safety standards and codes can still be harmonised — one of the cheapest available interventions, and one that gets harder the longer it is left.
