machines

The Korea Superconducting Tokamak Advanced Research device — the first tokamak built entirely with Nb₃Sn superconductors.

A long-pulse and high-temperature research workhorse: ion temperatures above 100 million K sustained for tens of seconds. Installed a tungsten divertor in 2023-24, making it directly relevant to ITER's tungsten-wall strategy.

KSTAR is also a principal site for AI plasma control research, in collaboration with Princeton and DeepMind — including the tearing-instability avoidance work published in Nature in 2024.

South Korea is an ITER Member at 9.1%, manufactures vacuum vessel sectors, completed its final ITER component in July 2026, and plans K-DEMO as the follow-on. In August 2026 President Lee Jae Myung named fusion one of seven strategic emerging technologies.
