The neutron multiplier the field is quietly walking away from. Beryllium was ITER's original first-wall material and remains a candidate multiplier in solid breeding blankets, but its toxicity, its activation behaviour and its supply constraints have pushed several designs toward lead instead.

Why it was chosen#

Beryllium multiplies neutrons via (n,2n), raising the Tritium Breeding Ratio (TBR) above what lithium alone can achieve. It is also low-Z, so the plasma-facing penalty for sputtered beryllium entering the plasma is far smaller than for a high-Z metal — a core beryllium influx costs little radiated power, where tungsten above roughly 10⁻⁵ concentration will Quench the discharge.

It had a second, unplanned virtue: beryllium getters oxygen. That kept ITER's impurity levels down without a dedicated system.

Why it is being abandoned#

  • Chronic beryllium disease. Beryllium dust causes an irreversible granulomatous lung condition at very low exposures. Handling demands stringent containment and medical surveillance, which raises cost across manufacturing, installation and — critically — remote maintenance and decommissioning, where activated beryllium dust is simultaneously a radiological and a chemical hazard.
  • It activates to long-lived isotopes. This complicates the claim that fusion waste decays in 50–100 years, and it is one of the impurity-driven problems described in Fusion Waste: Classification, Clearance and Disposal. A single plant may hold 300–400 t.
  • Supply. Beryllium is among the constrained materials examined in Critical Materials and Supply Chains.

Several designs therefore prefer lead as the multiplier — see Lithium-Lead Eutectic (PbLi), which is abundant, cheap and comparatively non-toxic.

The consequence ITER did not plan for#

ITER's 2023 change of first-wall armour from beryllium to tungsten removed its built-in oxygen control. Impurities rose, and a wall-conditioning requirement appeared that the original design never had — which is why ITER now needs the Boronization and Wall Conditioning system described there. Removing a material removed a function nobody had listed as a requirement.
