The paper that puts a number on what every fuel-cycle model in this vault had left out. Full text held; CC BY 4.0, version of record. Received 12 September 2024, accepted 22 January 2025.

The omission it closes#

Its own predecessor and Abdou et al. both track tritium as it flows. Neither tracks tritium that stops — held in first wall, divertors and breeding blanket by trapping. The authors state plainly that no state-of-the-art fuel-cycle model considers it, and that two effects compound it: traps created by irradiation damage, and the component replacements that carry trapped inventory out of the plant.

The novelty claimed is integrating three things into one system-level model — a physics-based trapping model, the evolution of damage-induced traps, and component replacement.

The result, for an ARC-class plant#

Warning

Trapped inventory is not a rounding error Tritium inventory in the first wall and vacuum vessel rises by a factor of 10³–10⁴ once trapping, damage-induced traps and replacement are accounted for.

Carried through the fuel cycle, that slows the whole system:

Quantity Change against a no-trapping scenario
Doubling time +50%
Start-up inventory +30%
Required TBR +2–5%

Why that uplift matters to this vault#

A 2–5% uplift on required TBR is small in isolation and decisive in context. The declared ST-E1 breeding values recorded in Declared TBR Values and Their Conflicts span 1.085–1.146, and the 2023 paper bounds maximum achievable TBR at 1.1–1.2. Applying this correction to a requirement already sitting inside that band moves the requirement toward — or past — what the design achieves. No source in this vault has done that calculation for ST-E1, and this page does not do it either: the uplift is derived for an ARC-class plant with a FLiBe immersion blanket, not for a liquid-lithium spherical tokamak, and transferring it would be inference, not evidence.

What it does not establish#

  • Nothing measured. A modelling result for an unbuilt plant; no fuel cycle has operated.
  • ARC-class only. The 10³–10⁴ figure and all three percentage changes are ARC-class.
  • No accountancy claim. Trapped tritium is modelled, not measured — and Tritium Accountancy records that no instrument exists for the blanket term at all.
