The whole energy system

What this reference supports

A fusion plant must power its own systems, including heating, magnets and their cooling, pumps, vacuum equipment, and fuel handling. Those loads affect the electricity available for export.

Where the evidence stops

Plasma gain, target gain, total electrical input, and exported electricity use different accounting boundaries. A better control metric does not by itself measure an improvement in plant energy efficiency. This brief introduces the distinction without assigning a plant-wide gain or efficiency value.
