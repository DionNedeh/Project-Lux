A self-organised, elongated plasmoid in which the plasma's own current reverses the applied field, creating closed field lines with no toroidal field at all.

Beta approaches 1 — plasma pressure nearly equals magnetic pressure. The highest of any configuration, and the reason the FRC is the only geometry in which Aneutronic Fusion (p-¹¹B) looks even remotely plausible.

Advantages#

  • Maximum magnetic field efficiency
  • Simple linear geometry with a natural divertor at each end
  • Translatable — the plasmoid can be formed in one place and moved to another
  • Natural Direct Energy Conversion

The weakness#

Stability. FRCs are theoretically vulnerable to tilt and rotational modes, and historically were short-lived. Confinement scaling is far less established than for tokamaks — the concept sits two to four orders of magnitude below tokamaks on achieved Triple Product.

Pursued by: TAE Technologies (beam-driven, targeting p-¹¹B) and Helion Energy (colliding, compressed, pulsed, with inductive Direct Energy Conversion).
