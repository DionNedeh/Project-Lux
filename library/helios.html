Thea Energy's deuterium-tritium planar-coil Stellarator power plant, existing as a preconceptual design only. It follows Canis (a magnet-array prototype, built) and Eos (a planned demonstrator). Evidence: Overview of the Helios Design: A Practical Planar Coil Stellarator Fusion Power Plant.

Design point#

A two-field-period quasi-axisymmetric equilibrium (Quasi-symmetry and Quasi-isodynamicity) with aspect ratio 4.5, major radius 8 m, minor radius 1.8 m, 6 T on axis and 500 m³ of plasma. It is designed to run ignited and steady-state — 10 MW of ECRH to start up, under 1 MW thereafter, with a roughly two-hour startup.

Quantity Value
Fusion power 958 MW
Total thermal power 1,094 MW
Net electric power ~390 MWe
Magnet operating temperature 20 K
Maximum field on coil 20 T
Idealised TBR 1.3
Capacity factor 88%

The coil set is 12 large plasma-encircling coils plus 324 smaller field-shaping coils, all planar and convex, all HTS. Individually controlling the shaping coils is what buys relaxed manufacturing tolerance and bootstrap-current control. Encircling coils are insulated with active Quench dumping to external resistors; shaping coils are partially insulated and self-protecting. The 20 T cap is set against achieved practice — 20.1 T in existing large-bore HTS magnets.

Architecture choices worth noting#

  • A toroidally continuous non-resonant X-point Divertor, tokamak-like, which the authors state is the first time a stellarator equilibrium has been explicitly designed to include one. Targets are 51,000 tessellated hexagonal tungsten tiles under an assumed 10 MW/m² heat flux, helium-jet cooled.
  • A Pb-17Li breeding blanket, 65% enriched in Lithium-6, in a uniform 50 cm layer with EUROFER97 structure and helium cooling.
  • A V-4Cr-4Ti first wall with tungsten armour, chosen over RAFM steel for a 15-full-power-year life — with an immature supply chain acknowledged as a contraindication.
  • Maintenance removes entire toroidal sectors from between the encircling coils, on one 84-day outage every two years.

The boundary#

Nothing about Helios is demonstrated. Confinement rests on ISS04 scaling with an enhancement factor of 1.4 — a value achieved in W7-X but not in this geometry — and beta is capped at 2.7% for conservatism. Set against pulsed EU-DEMO, which needs 8–14 primary coolant systems and a molten-salt store, Helios needs neither — the architectural dividend of steady state. See Balance of Plant and Overview of the Helios Design: A Practical Planar Coil Stellarator Fusion Power Plant for what the source does and does not establish.
