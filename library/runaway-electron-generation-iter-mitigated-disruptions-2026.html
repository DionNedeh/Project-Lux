The single most concerning technical result in this wiki, and one that receives far less attention than tritium or materials.

Modelling with the DREAM framework of ITER disruptions mitigated by Shattered Pellet Injection (SPI):

Megaampere-scale runaway currents are found in 15 MA discharges even in non-activated scenarios. While staggered injection outperforms single-stage — including in cases with radioactive runaway sources — no cases with acceptable runaway electron currents are found for a D-T plasma.

Why D-T is intrinsically worse#

Tritium beta decay and Compton scattering from activated structures continuously generate runaway seed electrons. Unlike hot-tail seeds, these cannot be removed by cooling the plasma faster — and every seed feeds the avalanche, whose gain scales exponentially with plasma current.

As of August 2026, ITER's disruption mitigation strategy has no demonstrated solution for its full-current D-T scenario. A concrete unsolved problem on the critical path of the world's largest fusion project.
