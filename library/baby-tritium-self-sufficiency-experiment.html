Direct measurement of tritium production in small breeder volumes — establishing the measurement techniques on which Tritium Breeding Ratio (TBR) validation depends.

Small-scale experiments of this kind are, at present, essentially all the experimental evidence there is: no integrated breeding blanket has ever operated, and ITER's Test Blanket Modules are the first in-device test, from 2039.

UNITY-3 and LIBRTI are the facilities that will close the gap sooner.
