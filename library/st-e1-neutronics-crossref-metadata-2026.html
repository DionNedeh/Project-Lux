Warning

CORRECTION 2026-11-02 — the 30-year magnet life does not survive the full text This page previously recorded, from the abstract, that the design "demonstrates feasibility of limiting neutron flux to the magnet systems to support a full power life of 30 years." The full text shows the baseline does not achieve it. Peak HTS fast flux is 9.5 × 10⁹ cm⁻² s⁻¹ against a stated < 4 × 10⁹ limit — 2.4× over — for 13 full-power years, not 30. A 2400 MW variant, tungsten boride at aspect ratio 2.31 with a 100 mm plasma–wall gap, reaches ~5.6 × 10⁹ and roughly 25 fpy. The abstract described an intent; the paper reports a shortfall against it.

Breeding, now with numbers#

MCNP 1.169 0.327
G4Tokamak 1.194 0.322

No TBR target is stated, and lithium enrichment is not mentioned — the breeder is covered in 086004. Inboard breeding is ruled out for lack of space; the outboard vertical shield reflects neutrons back to recover TBR. Both values sit above 086004's 1.146 baseline — Declared TBR Values and Their Conflicts.

What the full text settles#

Detail sits on the pages that should carry it; this is the index of it.

  • Nuclear data, previously open: ENDF/B-VIII.0 transport, FISPACT-II / TENDL-2019 activation — Nuclear Data and FENDL.
  • The MCNP benchmark has a metric: mean flux ratio 0.90 — Simulation, Codes and Nuclear Data.
  • Radiation damage: ≈ 50 dpa/fpy in centre-column tungsten carbide — Displacements per Atom (dpa).
  • Cryogenic margin: 18.5 kW against 20 kW — Superconductors and Magnet Engineering.
  • Materials: V-4Cr-4Ti shield box (Vanadium Alloys), F82H RAFM overhead shield, 316L(N)-IG vessel, K-doped tungsten armour.

What it still does not establish#

  • No neutron wall loading in MW m⁻², so the overview's figures remain uncross-checked; no cumulative fluence, only the fast-flux limit.
  • Nothing measured. Pre-concept simulation of an unbuilt plant, by its designers.
  • Activation remains a named problem: non-reduced-activation steels and plasma-facing components activate most in the machine.
