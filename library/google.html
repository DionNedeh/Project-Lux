economicsinstitutions

Fusion offtaker and investor.

  • 200 MW power purchase agreement with Commonwealth Fusion Systems — half the output of the first ARC plant, targeted for the Virginia grid in the early 2030s. The largest fusion offtake agreement
  • Direct equity investment in CFS, aligning interests through construction
  • Equity investment in TAE Technologies
  • Separately, Google Research funded a multi-year effort to test cold fusion claims with modern instrumentation, publishing in Nature (2019). It did not obtain reliable evidence — but established methodologies later built on by ARPA-E

Google's PPA matters beyond its megawatts: a long-term contract with an investment-grade counterparty converts merchant price risk into contracted revenue, which is the precondition for project debt on a first-of-a-kind plant. See The Investment Mechanics.
