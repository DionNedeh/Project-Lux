institutionssupply chain

Helium-3 supply company pursuing lunar extraction.

  • $500 million in helium-3 contracts to supply the US Department of Energy — Office of Fusion and fusion reactor companies
  • Partnered with NASA's Jet Propulsion Laboratory and Caltech for a robotic lunar mission — JPL overseeing robotic systems, instrumentation, data collection and mission operations
  • Targeting 2029 for a reconnaissance rover
  • 5 August 2026: strategic helium-3 supply partnership with TAE Technologies

Warning

The arithmetic has not changed since the 1980s Helium-3 concentrations in lunar regolith are on the order of parts per billion. Historical estimates run to roughly 100 million tonnes of regolith processed per tonne of helium-3.

The contracts and the JPL partnership are real. The concentration problem is the obstacle — not launch cost, not robotics. Any fusion company whose fuel plan depends on lunar helium-3 has a plan that will not survive contact with a mining engineer.
