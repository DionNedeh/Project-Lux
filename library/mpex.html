The Material Plasma Exposure eXperiment at Oak Ridge National Laboratory — a linear plasma device for exposing candidate plasma-facing materials to reactor-relevant plasma fluxes.

A US Department of Energy — Office of Fusion roadmap near-term milestone: "Commission MPEX to deliver plasma exposure testing and launch complementary domestic high-heat-flux testing."

MPEX addresses the plasma side of plasma-material interaction; IFMIF-DONES and Fusion Prototypic Neutron Source (FPNS) address the neutron side. Neither addresses both simultaneously, which is what a real first wall experiences — and which the roadmap defers to the long term: "validate tungsten-based and liquid-metal PFCs under fusion-prototypic neutron, heat, and plasma flux."
