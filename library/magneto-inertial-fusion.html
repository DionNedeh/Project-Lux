Magneto-Inertial Fusion (MIF)

physics

The middle ground between magnetic and Inertial Confinement: embed a magnetic field in the fuel before compressing it.

The field suppresses thermal conduction to the wall and traps charged fusion products, so the fuel need not reach ICF densities to burn. This relaxes the compression requirement by orders of magnitude.

The appeal is that it targets the least-demanding corner of parameter space — roughly 10²⁴–10²⁸ m⁻³ for microseconds, rather than 10³¹/nanoseconds or 10²⁰/seconds.

Variants#

Variant Who
MagLIF — pulsed-power liner onto pre-magnetised, laser-preheated fuel Z Machine (Sandia)
Magnetized Target Fusion — plasma injected into a collapsing liquid metal cavity General Fusion
Pulser-driven ICF — coordinated electromagnetic pulses instead of lasers Pacific Fusion
Sheared-flow-stabilised Z-pinch and Sheared-Flow Stabilisation Zap Energy
Colliding compressed FRCs Helion Energy

9 of the 56 companies in the FIA survey pursue MIF. Each leading exponent solves a different engineering problem by construction — a liquid wall, direct recovery, no coils — which is why the category is worth watching even though its achieved performance sits well below tokamaks.
