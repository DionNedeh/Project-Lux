p + ¹¹B → 3 ⁴He, releasing 8.68 MeV entirely in charged particles.

The prize: no neutrons, no tritium, no breeding blanket, no activation, and Direct Energy Conversion without a steam cycle. Five of the 56 FIA-surveyed companies pursue it.

Why it is punishing#

  • The Coulomb barrier is five times higher (Z = 1 × 5); reactivity peaks near 600 keV
  • Critically, bremsstrahlung losses scale as Z_eff²n²√T. With boron's Z = 5 in the plasma, radiated power tends to exceed fusion power across most of the operating space

Net energy requires non-Maxwellian ion distributions, alpha channelling, or operation far from thermal equilibrium — all unproven at scale. Only FRCs, with β ≈ 1, make the pressure requirement even conceivable.

Status, August 2026#

  • HB11 Energy reported a ~10× increase in alpha yield at ELI Beamlines (Czechia), May 2026, using a chirped-pulse petawatt driver on a boron-hydride target
  • TAE Technologies has reached ~75 million K for ~30 ms — still 5–10× below what p-¹¹B needs, against a requirement near 3 billion K

No facility has demonstrated sustained aneutronic fusion, let alone breakeven. The endgame is genuinely attractive; the timelines quoted are not credible.
