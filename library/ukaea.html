The UK's fusion agency, at Culham. Operator of JET — Joint European Torus for forty years and now of its decommissioning.

The programme#

  • UK Fusion Energy Strategy, March 2026 — "A new energy revolution" — with over £2.5 billion committed across five years
  • STEP — a prototype fusion power plant at West Burton, delivered through UK Industrial Fusion Solutions Ltd
  • LIBRTI — £220 m, a global first-of-a-kind lithium breeding tritium facility
  • H3AT — tritium processing at plant scale, with Eni
  • RACE — remote handling and robotics
  • MAST-Upgrade — the Super-X Divertor testbed
  • JET — Joint European Torus Decommissioning and Repurposing — running to ~2040
  • Culham AI Growth Zone — £125 m, including £45 m for Sunrise, a 1.4 MW fusion-dedicated supercomputer

Regulation#

The Energy Act 2023 formally excluded fusion from nuclear site licensing. Fusion facilities are regulated by the Environment Agency and HSE, not the ONR — making the UK the most permissive major jurisdiction, deliberately.

12 August 2026: Dennis Whyte appointed Chief Executive.

The Tritium Fuel Cycle division#

Added 2026-10-19. UKAEA runs a dedicated Tritium Fuel Cycle division, which with LIBRTI is developing the analytical techniques that tritium accountancy lacks — HERA for real-time gas-stream analysis and THALIA for tritium trapped in liquid breeders, both at low technology readiness level (Development of Analytical Techniques for Tritium Accountancy Around the Fusion Fuel Cycle (HERA and THALIA)).

This is the only live accountancy instrument-development programme in this vault, and it sits alongside H3AT in tritium handling.

A third targeted bet: vanadium#

Added 2026-11-02. UKAEA's manufacturing group at Rotherham is building a V-4Cr-4Ti strategy covering R&D, supply chain and industrialisation (UKAEA Vanadium Strategy (LIBRTI conference presentation)) — the first source here to treat that supply chain at all. Its long-term bets: a V44/liquid-lithium self-cooled blanket as "the optimal solution for commercial fusion powerplants", and a domestic UK vanadium supply chain. Qualification is being attempted by a novel digital route, Bayesian optimisation with EPRI engaged for regulatory acceptance, partnered with ORNL, Tokamak Energy and ATI. A strategy poster, not a qualified material.

Assessment#

Small in absolute terms, exceptionally well targeted. The UK has invested in the three things nobody else is adequately funding — tritium breeding, tritium handling, and now the structural alloy that liquid-lithium breeding depends on — plus a genuine prototype plant with construction money and a site.
