Project purpose · The whole energy system

What this reference supports

The Lux engineering synthesis examines the systems around a fusion plasma: materials exposed to neutrons, heat removal, fuel availability, component maintenance, and plant operation. These interact, so progress in one area does not settle the whole engineering case.

Where the evidence stops

These are research and engineering questions, not claims that Lux operates a plant or has solved them. This short brief is a guided entry into the repository; it is not a fresh technical audit of the full synthesis.
