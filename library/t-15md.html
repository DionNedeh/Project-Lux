Upgraded Russian Tokamak at the Kurchatov Institute in Moscow. It was formally launched in May 2021 and produced its first high-temperature plasma in March 2023, according to the ITER item named in References and Bibliography.

T-15MD is an operating research machine, not the proposed Russian commercial prototype. Its immediate programme is to reach design parameters and support work on reactor-relevant plasma operation and technology. Russia’s planned next step is the separate TRT device at TRINITI, whose preliminary design is complete.

The distinction matters because Russian programme descriptions often list operating, upgraded and proposed machines together. T-15MD has demonstrated plasma operation; TRT is designed. Russia also remains an ITER member and supplier despite the geopolitical rupture after 2022, so the machine sits within both a national development chain and a surviving international technical network.
