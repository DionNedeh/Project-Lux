machines

The US flagship and the most flexible tokamak in the world. R = 1.66 m, a = 0.67 m, B₀ = 2.2 T, I_p = 2 MA, operated by General Atomics.

Copper coils — so pulsed, seconds not minutes — but with extraordinarily comprehensive diagnostics and shaping control. DIII-D's value is that you can ask it almost any physics question and get an answer quickly.

What it has produced#

  • Negative Triangularity operation, explored more thoroughly here than anywhere
  • Shattered Pellet Injection (SPI) — first demonstrated at DIII-D in 2010, now ITER's disruption mitigation system
  • Pedestal Super H-mode, accessed experimentally
  • DeepMind's tearing-instability-avoidance reinforcement learning (Nature, 2024)
  • A very large fraction of the world's transport, pedestal and ELM-control database

The US Department of Energy — Office of Fusion roadmap explicitly names DIII-D as a facility to be sustained and connected to AI/HPC pipelines for real-time digital-twin capability.
