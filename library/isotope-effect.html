physicsrisks

An unsolved problem hiding in plain sight, and a real uncertainty in every reactor projection.

Simple gyro-Bohm theory says confinement should degrade with increasing isotope mass. Experiments consistently show it improves: deuterium plasmas confine better than hydrogen, and D-T better still.

This has never been fully explained.

It matters because every reactor projection is extrapolated from deuterium experiments to D-T operation, and the direction and magnitude of the isotope correction is therefore an open term in the extrapolation. JET — Joint European Torus's D-T campaigns were among the very few opportunities to measure it directly.

The effect also runs the other way for the L-H Transition and Power Threshold power threshold, which scales roughly inversely with isotope mass — hydrogen worst. That is bad news for ITER's early campaigns, which run H and D precisely to avoid activation, and which will therefore attempt H-mode access under the hardest conditions.
