A linear rather than toroidal configuration: a field with strong magnetic "plugs" at each end. The magnetic-moment invariant makes a converging field reflect particles moving along the axis.

Advantages#

  • Simple geometry — cylindrical vessel, simple coils, straightforward maintenance access, easy to segment for replacement. A decisive advantage over a torus threaded through 3D coils
  • High beta
  • Direct Energy Conversion is natural — charged particles escaping the ends can be decelerated against an electrostatic field

Why it was abandoned, and why it returned#

End losses. Particles in the velocity-space loss cone escape immediately. This is intrinsic, and it is why the US cancelled MFTF-B in 1986 — on the day construction was completed, without a single plasma shot.

REBCO High-Temperature Superconductor changed the arithmetic. Higher achievable mirror ratios directly shrink the loss cone. WHAM achieved 17 T on a confined plasma in July 2024 — the highest field ever applied to a fusion plasma — and Realta Fusion is commercialising it.

A textbook case of a dormant rather than dead concept: killed by budget, revived by a conductor.
