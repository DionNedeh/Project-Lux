  1. Migration into private capital

The most important financial innovation in fusion — and it originated in the public sector.

US Department of Energy — Office of Fusion's Milestone-Based Fusion Development Program (May 2023, eight companies) was modelled explicitly on NASA's Commercial Orbital Transportation Services. Payment is made on demonstrated milestones, not submitted invoices. The government specifies an outcome; the company chooses how to reach it; money moves when the outcome is verified.

It has produced verifiable results: Thea Energy became the first awardee to complete a full pilot plant design review, and DOE approved Xcimer Energy's Athena preconceptual design in August 2026.

Migration into private capital#

Pacific Fusion secured a $1 billion Series A with milestone-based tranches. The market observation is direct: investors are increasingly demanding milestones.

General Fusion's LM26 programme is structured the same way — three defined milestones: 1 keV, 10 keV, and achieving the Lawson criterion, executing through 2028. (LM26 has reached 0.72 keV.)

Why it is healthy#

  • It prices technical risk explicitly — miss a milestone, lose the tranche
  • It forces falsifiable public targets — you cannot tranche against a vague objective
  • It caps the cost of failure — a company stalling at milestone two has consumed one tranche, not a full round

It also transfers the sector's biggest problem into the open: every milestone schedule is a public statement now accumulating a track record.
