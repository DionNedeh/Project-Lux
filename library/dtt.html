machines

A fully Italian experimental device under construction at the ENEA Research Centre in Frascati — the largest magnetic confinement facility under construction in Europe after ITER.

Part of the EUROfusion programme, DTT exists to study alternative solutions to the DEMO thermal load problem: advanced magnetic configurations and divertors based on liquid metals.

2026 progress#

  • The first 170 GHz / 1 MW Gyrotron and ECRH for DTT's ECRH system passed acceptance testing at the FALCON facility in Lausanne: 1 MW for 100-second pulses, 95% reliability (19 of 20 pulses), pre-series tube from THALES at 1.02 MW and 40% efficiency
  • July 2026: power supplies for the divertor coils arrived at Frascati

The gyrotron procurement is a joint F4E–DTT effort serving both ITER and DTT — a rare instance of two major programmes sharing a component line.
