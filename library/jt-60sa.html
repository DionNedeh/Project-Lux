The largest superconducting tokamak in operation. Built jointly under the EU-Japan Broader Approach — part of the compensation package for Japan losing the ITER site bid.

R = 2.96 m, a = 1.18 m, B₀ = 2.25 T, I_p = 5.5 MA, plasma volume 135 m³.

Its purpose is to explore ITER- and DEMO-relevant scenarios ahead of ITER, particularly high-beta steady-state operation.

Status, August 2026#

Upgraded since 2024 with a new carbon-armoured first wall and divertor plus in-vessel plasma control coils. Commissioning of new equipment began 27 February 2026. The next experimental campaign starts at the end of 2026 and runs roughly six months.

The ITER-relevant result#

Achieved first plasma under a low toroidal inductive electric field of 0.15 V/m — deliberately so, because ITER's superconducting central solenoid cannot supply the high loop voltages that copper-coil tokamaks use for breakdown. Plasma start-up at low inductive E-field is a research topic in its own right.
