engineeringphysics

The only known method for making a reactor Divertor survivable.

Deliberately radiate the exhaust power away in the divertor volume before it reaches a surface, by seeding impurities (nitrogen, neon, argon) and operating at high neutral density. Plasma-neutral interactions then remove power, momentum and particles simultaneously, and the ion flux to the target collapses.

The structural tension#

The core must be hot and clean. The divertor must be cold and dirty. They are separated by perhaps a metre.

Whether a detached divertor is compatible with a high-performance Burning Plasma core at reactor scale is genuinely unresolved, and it is on ITER's critical question list.

The same tension recurs elsewhere: Super H-mode may not be compatible with reactor densities and detachment; impurity seeding that protects the divertor also dilutes and cools the core; and Burn Control must be maintained through all of it.

Latent-space surrogate models are now being used to put fast approximations of expensive edge simulations inside real-time detachment control loops.
