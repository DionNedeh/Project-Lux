institutions

Founded as Project Matterhorn in 1951, when Lyman Spitzer invented the Stellarator. Home of TFTR, which still holds the Triple Product record set in 1995.

Current work spans stellarator theory and optimisation, spherical tokamaks (NSTX-U), AI plasma control, and computation (XGC).

Notably, PPPL launched a four-year registered apprenticeship programme for technicians, open to anyone with a high school diploma — arguably the most important workforce initiative in the sector, because fusion does not only need PhDs. It needs vacuum technicians, cryogenic operators, precision welders, remote-handling operators and radiological control staff. See People, Institutions and Public Trust.
