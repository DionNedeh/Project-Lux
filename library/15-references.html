Notes on sourcing#

What was used and how. Primary documents (the FIA report, the DOE roadmap, arXiv papers) were downloaded and their text extracted directly; figures quoted from them are read from the source rather than from press coverage. Where only press coverage exists — as for several 2026 company milestones — the claim is attributed to the reporting outlet and flagged as a company statement where relevant.

Known conflicts left unresolved in the text:

  • TAE's Copernicus device — some sources state it has been skipped entirely in favour of Da Vinci; others still list it for a net-energy demonstration later this decade.
  • ENN's funding — the FIA reports $720 m declared; press reporting has cited $400 m.
  • Startorus — $138.9 m appears as a declared total while the FIA's raise chart shows $220 m for the year.
  • SPARC first plasma — sources from early 2026 still say 2026; the July 2026 assembly status and CFS statements indicate 2027.
  • JET's 1997 Q — the universally quoted 0.67 is a transient peak. Wurzel and Hsu's harmonised dataset gives 0.27 for the same shot under a consistent definition. Both figures appear in this dossier, labelled. See Document 26.
  • ENN funding — resolved in Part II as an FIA-declared $720 m against press reporting of $400 m; the FIA figure is used, with the discrepancy noted in Document 07.

Correction made after the second research pass. Round 1 gave JET's DTE3 69 MJ shot a Q_sci of ~0.33. The primary dataset (Wurzel & Hsu, Table IV, shot 104522) gives 0.37; 0.33 is the value for the 2021 DTE2 59 MJ shot. Corrected in Document 02.

Coverage gap. The FIA states explicitly that its Chinese coverage is incomplete because several companies with reported government and private funding did not respond to the survey. Any comparison of national totals in this dossier understates Chinese private activity.
