icfmachines

The largest pulsed-power facility in the world. Roughly 20 megaamperes delivered to the load in about 100 nanoseconds, from around 20 MJ of stored energy.

Z drives MagLIF — Magnetized Liner Inertial Fusion — in which the current implodes a metal liner onto pre-magnetised, laser-preheated D-T fuel. It appears as a distinct experimental line in the Wurzel-Hsu Lawson dataset.

It also generates the most intense laboratory X-ray source on Earth, used for Z-pinch-driven hohlraums and, like National Ignition Facility, for stockpile stewardship — which is what funds it.

Its architecture — Marx generators, water-dielectric pulse-forming lines, magnetically insulated transmission lines — is being superseded for commercial purposes by Linear Transformer Drivers, which are more compact, higher repetition rate and modular. See Heavy Ion Fusion and Pulsed Power and Pacific Fusion.
