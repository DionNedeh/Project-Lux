magnets

The 20 tesla model coil test of September 2021 — arguably the single most consequential event in fusion between 2010 and 2022, and it was not a plasma result.

Commonwealth Fusion Systems and MIT tested a large-bore REBCO High-Temperature Superconductor toroidal-field model coil to 20 T, using the NINT no-insulation, no-twist architecture. It converted the high-field compact tokamak from a paper argument into an engineering fact.

It directly precipitated CFS's $1.8 bn Series B — at the time the largest private fusion raise ever — and the wave of investment that followed. See The B⁴ Scaling and Quench.
