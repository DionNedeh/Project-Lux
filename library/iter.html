The largest scientific collaboration in history: seven Members representing 35 nations, building a Tokamak at Saint-Paul-lès-Durance in Provence.

Parameter Value
R / a 6.2 m / 2.0 m
Plasma volume 840 m³
Toroidal field 5.3 T
Plasma current 15 MA
Design goal 500 MW for 400 s at Q_sci = 10
Magnet stored energy ~51 GJ
Machine mass ~23,000 t

It is not a power plant. It generates no electricity. Its purpose is to demonstrate a Burning Plasma at reactor scale and test the nuclear technologies a plant would need.

Cost sharing#

EU ~45.5%, and China, India, Japan, Korea, Russia, USA ~9.1% each. Roughly 90% of contributions are in kind — completed components rather than money — which is what makes a 35-nation project fundable and is also its structural flaw. Cost: most plausibly €20–45 bn depending entirely on accounting convention.

Baseline 2024#

The old "First Plasma 2025" staged approach was abandoned after the vessel and thermal shield defects (Failure Case Studies).

Milestone Date
Start of Research Operation 2034
D-D operation 2035
Full magnetic energy 2036
Start of D-T Operation 2039

June 2026: on budget and on schedule against Baseline 2024. Magnet system complete; sector modules installing.

What it will and will not prove#

Will: burning plasma physics, integrated operation at scale, the first ever tritium breeding test (Test Blanket Modules), licensing precedent as the first fusion device licensed as a nuclear installation.

Will not: net electricity, tritium self-sufficiency, materials for a power plant (<2 dpa lifetime against 15 dpa/yr), or economics.
