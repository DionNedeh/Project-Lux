The problems that will actually decide whether fusion becomes an energy source. Long-form: Engineering Challenges.

The field's own ranking, from The Global Fusion Industry Report 2026: neutron-resilient materials and tritium self-sufficiency top the long-term challenge list. Plasma science ranks seventh.

The four Tier-1 problems#

  1. Materials — Helium Embrittlement · Displacements per Atom (dpa) · Reduced-Activation Steels · Why Fission Data Cannot Qualify Fusion Materials · Activation Products · Beryllium
  2. Fuel cycle — Tritium Breeding Ratio (TBR) · Tritium Burn Fraction · Tritium Start-up Inventory · Tritium Accountancy · Direct Internal Recycling (DIR) · Lithium-6 · Helium Ash · Lithium-Lead Eutectic (PbLi)
  3. Exhaust — Divertor · Detachment · Super-X Divertor · Tungsten Plasma-Facing Components
  4. Availability and RAMI — the most economically decisive parameter, and the least analysed

Magnets#

REBCO High-Temperature Superconductor · Quench · The B⁴ Scaling · Canis — planar HTS array manufacturing and control proof

Subsystems#

Gyrotron and ECRH · Neutral Beam Injection (NBI) · Pellet Injection · Boronization and Wall Conditioning · Balance of Plant · Primary Heat Transfer System (PHTS) · Recirculating Power · Direct Energy Conversion · Pulsed Operation and Thermal Storage

Computation#

Nuclear Data and FENDL — the uncertainty under every TBR calculation

The facilities that would close the gaps#

IFMIF-DONES · Fusion Prototypic Neutron Source (FPNS) · UNITY-3 · LIBRTI · H3AT · MPEX · NBTF — SPIDER and MITICA · RACE · JET Remote Handling and MASCOT

None of the first two operates. See Open Problems and Risks.
