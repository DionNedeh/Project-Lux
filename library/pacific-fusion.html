The fastest-funded newcomer in the sector's history, founded by a team including former Meta CTO Mike Schroepfer and physicists from the National Ignition Facility and Z Machine lineages.

Uses coordinated electromagnetic pulses rather than lasers to drive inertial confinement. Pulsed power converts wall-plug electricity to load energy at over 50% efficiency, versus a laser's ~10% aspiration — the strategic case for the whole category. See Heavy Ion Fusion and Pulsed Power.

Milestone-gated funding#

Pacific Fusion's distinguishing practice is a $1 billion Series A with milestone-based tranches — capital committed but released only on demonstrated technical milestones, published in advance. See Milestone Tranching.

Demonstrated#

  • June 2026: second milestone set achieved — a scaled module prototype delivering more than 440 GW of peak output power at approximately 1.1 MV in ultra-fast pulses
  • 20 July 2026: with Lawrence Livermore National Laboratory, surpassed 3,000 successful Sirius pulsed-power prototype tests

That second item is the more informative one. 3,000 successful shots on a prototype module is a reliability statistic — and reliability at high shot count is exactly what a pulsed plant needs and what no laser system has demonstrated.

State sovereign capital. The New Mexico State Investment Council, a state fund built on oil and gas revenue, was reported in March 2026 to be backing a $1 bn Pacific Fusion project near Albuquerque — a resource-revenue fund diversifying into the technology that would displace its own revenue base. Single-outlet trade reporting. See State Capital and the Public Balance Sheet.
