internationalmachines

The Tokamak Chauffage Alfvén Brésilien at the University of São Paulo, described in the Round 3 sources as the largest Tokamak in the Southern Hemisphere.

TCABR was built at EPFL in Switzerland, operated there from 1980 to 1992, then was transferred and reassembled in Brazil, where it has operated since 1999 with periodic upgrades. The 1980 frontmatter date records the machine’s original first operation; Brazilian operation began later. Its history is a useful counterexample to the assumption that fusion capability spreads only through new billion-dollar facilities.

A second-hand European machine received a multi-decade second life and became a training and experimental platform for another continent. The current corpus does not support reactor-scale performance claims for TCABR, and none are made. Its importance is capacity building, diagnostics and the institutional continuity described in Fusion's Global Periphery.
