engineering

An alternative Divertor geometry that lengthens the magnetic connection length to the target and increases the wetted area, spreading the exhaust power over more surface and promoting Detachment.

MAST-Upgrade is the world's leading Super-X testbed. Its 2025 Nature Energy result — first demonstration of Super-X exhaust control for transient heat load management in compact fusion reactors — is one of the more consequential exhaust results of the decade, precisely because the problem is most acute in compact designs.

The concept is central to the UK's STEP programme, which as a spherical tokamak has both a severe exhaust problem and limited space in which to solve it.

Related alternative configurations: the X-point radiator (XPR) and quasi-continuous exhaust (QCE), both of which JET — Joint European Torus demonstrated in actual D-T plasmas during DTE3.
