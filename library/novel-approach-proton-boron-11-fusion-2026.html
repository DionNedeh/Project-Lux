Recent work on the p-¹¹B fuel cycle.

The attraction is real — 8.68 MeV entirely in charged particles, no neutrons, no tritium, no breeding blanket, and Direct Energy Conversion without a steam cycle.

The obstacle is equally real: a Coulomb barrier five times higher, reactivity peaking near 600 keV, and bremsstrahlung losses scaling as Z_eff²n²√T with boron's Z = 5, such that radiated power tends to exceed fusion power across most of the operating space.

Related: the Frontiers in Nuclear Engineering (2026) evaluation of the Lawson criterion for p-¹¹B including ion temperature and bremsstrahlung effects.
