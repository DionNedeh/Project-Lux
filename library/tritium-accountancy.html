Knowing where every gram of tritium is, to a defensible uncertainty, in a system that leaks by design — the part of the fuel cycle whose instruments do not yet exist.

The structure#

A facility is divided into a Material Balance Area whose boundary is fully measured, subdivided into Material Sub-Accounts with Key Measurement Points. Each period an inventory is taken and the books closed by the inventory difference:

ID = SI + R − S − EI        σ²_ID = σ²_SI + σ²_R + σ²_S + σ²_EI

That uncertainty is the Limit of Error on Inventory Difference, which decides whether the result means anything (Tritium Accountancy in Fusion Systems).

Why fusion breaks it#

Three structural failures.

  1. The plant cannot stop. Eq. 1 assumes flow halts long enough to measure, and uncertainty on a continuously measured flow grows as √t, so receipt and shipment terms eventually dominate the inventory terms. The stated remedy — enlarge the MSA — buys validity by surrendering resolution.
  2. Retention is a residual, not a measurement. The balance assumes all tritium not accounted for elsewhere resides in the fusion chamber, so every error lands on the retained inventory.
  3. The fusion-specific terms have no instrument. Methods for consumption in the plasma and production in the blanket "would need to be developed" (Assessment of Challenges for Tritium Accountancy and Control in Fusion Energy Systems).

What can be measured, and by whom#

Laser Raman is most accurate at < 0.5%, calorimetry ~1%, environmental releases ~10% — figures from 2006. UKAEA's HERA and THALIA are building the missing instruments at low TRL. Detail: Vacuum, Wall Conditioning and the Tritium Plant §7.

A method for continuous operation#

Flynn and Larsen 2022 apply a Kalman Filter — the first such application — taking simulated sensor error from 64.37% to ≈ 0.001% in three days, inside the 1% standard.

Warning

It fails on exactly the error real sensors make Given accumulating bias the filter tracks the sensor's mean, not the true value — and the authors name vibration, ground loops and RF components as sources of precisely that. Simulated sensors throughout.

No regime requires it#

Tritium was a special nuclear material to the DOE until 2011; neither the NRC nor the IAEA treats it as one, so neither prescribes any requirement — yet the 10–20% breeding surplus is the only divertible stock a plant makes (Military, Dual-Use and Security).

Status#

Never performed on a fusion power plant, because none exists. ITER's in-process inventory is expected at ~1 kg — not the same quantity as start-up inventory.
