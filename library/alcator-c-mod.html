historymachines

MIT's high-field, compact tokamak — the direct intellectual ancestor of SPARC.

Set the record for plasma pressure in a magnetically confined plasma: 2.05 atmospheres, on its final day of operation in September 2016.

C-Mod's entire research programme was an argument that The B⁴ Scaling means high field beats large size. Commonwealth Fusion Systems was founded to prove it. Its 1160930033 shot (2016) reached a Triple Product of 7.4 × 10¹⁹ at a density of 5.5 × 10²⁰ m⁻³ — extraordinary density for its size.
