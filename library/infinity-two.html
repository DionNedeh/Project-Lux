machines

Type One Energy's optimised Stellarator power plant: 350 MW, sited with the Tennessee Valley Authority at a former coal facility, targeted mid-2030s.

Notable for having published a complete physics basis including breeder blanket and tritium fuel-cycle feasibility in the Journal of Plasma Physics — a level of public design detail rare for a private company, and one of the signals distinguishing serious efforts from pitch decks.

A US public utility hosting a stellarator at a retired coal plant is also a meaningful siting and political precedent — see Grid Integration and Plant Operations.
