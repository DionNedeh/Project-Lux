The high-confinement regime. Above a threshold heating power a tokamak plasma spontaneously reorganises into a state with roughly double the energy confinement time.

Discovered on ASDEX in 1982 by Fritz Wagner, initially met with disbelief, then reproduced everywhere. It is the baseline operating scenario for ITER, SPARC, EU DEMO and essentially every reactor design — arguably the single most valuable empirical result in magnetic fusion.

Mechanism#

At the plasma edge, sheared E×B flow tears apart turbulent eddies. Turbulence generates the flow via Reynolds stress; the flow suppresses the turbulence; suppression steepens the pressure gradient; the gradient sustains the flow. A bifurcation in a self-regulating predator-prey system.

The result is the Pedestal — a narrow edge transport barrier that sets the whole plasma's pressure.

The cost#

The pedestal periodically collapses in edge-localised modes. Uncontrolled Type-I ELMs would destroy a reactor divertor.

Duration record: EAST, 1,066 seconds of steady-state H-mode, January 2025.
