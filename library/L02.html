What this reference supports

The study uses historical machine data to train a dynamics model, then trains a reinforcement-learning controller in that simulator. It reports a 400 ms trajectory at a 1 kHz control rate on HL-3.

Where the evidence stops

This is bounded experimental control. It does not establish full-discharge management, long-duration reliability, or safe behavior in every off-normal condition.

Source & provenance

Wu, N. et al. High-fidelity data-driven dynamics model for reinforcement learning-based control in HL-3 tokamak. Communications Physics 8, 393 (2025).
