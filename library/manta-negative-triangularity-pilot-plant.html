A high-field compact fusion pilot plant design study: Modular, Adjustable, Negative-Triangularity ARC.

Its distinguishing choice is Negative Triangularity — reversing the plasma cross-section curvature to obtain good confinement without an H-mode Pedestal and therefore without ELMs, removing an entire failure mode by geometry rather than by control.

Claimed overnight cost: $3.4 billion, inside the NASEM threshold of $5 bn for a viable pilot plant. See Fusion LCOE and Capital Cost for why design-study costs cluster at the optimistic end of a thirty-fold range.
