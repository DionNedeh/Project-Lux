The US Advanced Research Projects Agency – Energy. In fusion its role is targeted, high-risk intervention rather than programme funding.

  • SCALEUP: $20 million to Thea Energy (9 July 2026) to establish the first US production line for modular REBCO High-Temperature Superconductor HTS magnets — a direct attack on the sector's tightest physical bottleneck
  • CHADWICK — fusion materials programme
  • LENR programme — $10 million across eight projects (February 2023), explicitly framed as an attempt to "break the stalemate" and prove or debunk low-energy nuclear reactions. See The Graveyard

ARPA-E also funds SHINE Technologies and appears across the sector's collaboration lists.
