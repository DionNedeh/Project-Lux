economicsinstitutions

Counterparty to the world's first fusion power purchase agreement, signed with Helion Energy in 2023.

Terms: at least 50 MW after a one-year ramp, from Helion's Orion plant at Malaga, Washington, targeted 2028.

Its significance is not the capacity but the precedent — an investment-grade buyer performing technical diligence and signing a long-term contract for fusion electricity, years before any machine had demonstrated net energy. It established the model that Google's 200 MW ARC agreement followed.

As of May 2026 hyperscalers had committed to over 9.8 GW across 13 nuclear projects, Microsoft at 835 MW plus fusion. See Economics and Markets.
