machinesoperations

Operating spherical Tokamak at the Ioffe Institute in St Petersburg. It appears in the Wurzel–Hsu Lawson dataset for 2019 and in the Russian machine inventories in Magnetic Confinement Devices and National Programs.

Its most consequential recent role in this corpus is operational rather than record-setting. A 2025 Physics of Plasmas paper named in References and Bibliography reports inter-shot boronization on Globus-M2: carborane is injected between discharges, using comparatively simple vacuum hardware, to maintain a low-impurity wall without stopping for a conventional glow-discharge campaign. The reported method outperformed conventional boronization and enabled longer experimental sessions.

That is a demonstrated machine result, not a general guarantee. It gives Boronization and Wall Conditioning a real-device precedent while leaving scale-up to machines such as ITER—with kilometre-scale gas lines, many injection points and tritium-plant interfaces—as a separate engineering problem.
