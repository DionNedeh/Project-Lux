Modular, Adjustable, Negative-Triangularity ARC — a high-field compact fusion pilot plant design study, NASEM-compliant.

Its distinguishing choice is Negative Triangularity, which gives good confinement without an H-mode Pedestal and therefore without ELMs — removing an entire failure mode by geometry rather than by control.

Claimed overnight cost: $3.4 billion — inside the NASEM threshold of $5 bn for a viable pilot plant. See Fusion LCOE and Capital Cost for why design-study costs cluster at the optimistic end of a thirty-fold range.

Full design paper held locally: MANTA: A Negative-Triangularity NASEM-Compliant Fusion Pilot Plant.
