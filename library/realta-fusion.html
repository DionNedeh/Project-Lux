Madison, Wisconsin — headquartered at the former Oscar Mayer site, selected July 2026. Spun out of UW-Madison.

Commercialising the Magnetic Mirror, revived by REBCO High-Temperature Superconductor magnets after thirty dormant years.

Two firsts#

  • WHAM: 17 tesla on a confined plasma (15 July 2024) — the highest field ever applied to a fusion plasma
  • 19 June 2026: first commercial fusion company to demonstrate Direct Energy Conversion of plasma energy to electricity

Roadmap and supply chain#

Anvil (demonstrator) → Hammir (commercial successor) → first-of-a-kind plants mid-2030s. Commonwealth Fusion Systems is designing and building the magnets under a strategic partnership announced April 2026.

Realta explicitly targets industrial process heat before grid electricity — rational for a compact, siteable device with direct conversion, and a way to avoid the thermal-cycle penalty entirely.

A US Department of Energy — Office of Fusion Milestone Program awardee.
