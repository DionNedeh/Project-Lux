The most aggressive schedule in fusion and the most polarising company in it. Everett, Washington.

Collides two FRCs at over a million miles per hour and compresses them magnetically. As the fused plasma expands against the field it induces current directly back into the coils — Direct Energy Conversion, no steam cycle. Targets D-³He, breeding its own helium-3 from D-D side reactions.

Status#

  • Polaris reached 150 million °C (February 2026) and is the first privately funded machine to operate with D-T fuel; running nearly every day
  • Orion, the commercial plant, under construction at Malaga, Washington
  • World's first fusion PPA (Microsoft, 2023): ≥50 MW after a one-year ramp, targeted 2028
  • Valuation $15.5 bn; $465 m raised in the survey year

The debate#

Helion publishes far less peer-reviewed detail than Commonwealth Fusion Systems or Type One Energy, and prominent plasma physicists question whether its pulsed direct-recovery efficiency claims survive contact with real losses.

Against that: it has a signed offtake agreement with a counterparty that performed technical diligence, it is operating a D-T machine daily, and it is building a plant. Both facts are real; this dossier does not adjudicate between them.
