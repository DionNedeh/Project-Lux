machinesphysics

Medium-sized, all-tungsten wall, at Max Planck Institute for Plasma Physics Garching — and the direct descendant of the machine where H-mode was discovered in 1982 by Fritz Wagner.

AUG remains one of the most scientifically productive tokamaks in the world. Much of the current understanding of:

  • Tungsten plasma-facing components and their impurity behaviour
  • Edge-Localised Mode (ELM) control by resonant magnetic perturbations
  • Detachment physics
  • Flux pumping and the sawtooth-free hybrid scenario

comes from it. Its Shattered Pellet Injection (SPI) and Neoclassical Tearing Mode (NTM) work is also widely used, and it is the reference machine for chemical trapping of diborane in Boronization and Wall Conditioning.

Its 2016 shot 32305 appears in the Wurzel-Hsu dataset at a Triple Product of 2.2 × 10¹⁹.
