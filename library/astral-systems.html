companiesmarkets

Bristol. Sells neutrons today, not electricity — the clearest example of the revenue-before-fusion model.

What it actually does#

  • Offers large-volume, continuously operated DD and DT irradiation services to commercial customers
  • Has completed hundreds of hours of commercial irradiation campaigns on tritium breeder blanket materials including solid lithium, FLiBe and composite breeders — directly serving the sector's hardest problem
  • Validated production of Cu-64, Cu-67, I-131, Tb-161, Sc-44, Sc-47 — several of them emerging therapeutic radionuclides with real clinical demand and constrained supply
  • Built a 150-tonne shielded vault for its high-flux DT volumetric neutron source; campaigns bookable directly
  • Berkeley, UK high-flux DT facility opening Q4 2026; further sites under consideration in Brazil, Canada, Sweden, USA and Germany

Partners: UK Atomic Energy Authority, Bristol, Bangor, McMaster, TU Munich, King's College London, IPEN.

Why it matters to everyone else: commercial DT neutron services are a partial, imperfect, immediately available substitute for the missing fusion-spectrum irradiation facilities — see Why Fission Data Cannot Qualify Fusion Materials. A market response to a public infrastructure gap.
