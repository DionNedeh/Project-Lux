The safety case that underwrites the regulatory model: Decay Heat — why fusion is regulated on the materials it handles rather than as a reactor. See also Fusion Waste: Classification, Clearance and Disposal.

The numbers that matter#

  • Fusion LCOE and Capital Cost — $1,400–43,000/kW FOAK spread; mature LCOE $110–145/MWh, and the independent analysis calls even that optimistic
  • Availability and RAMI — inversely proportional to LCOE, and never demonstrated
  • Recirculating Power — why Q_eng > 1 needs Q_sci of 10–30+
  • Milestone Tranching — the sector's most important financial innovation
