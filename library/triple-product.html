dataphysics

The field's single most useful figure of merit: peak ion density × peak ion temperature × energy confinement time.

For D-T ignition the idealised minimum is roughly 3 × 10²¹ keV·m⁻³·s, occurring near T ≈ 14 keV. Real plasmas with peaked profiles and impurities need 3–5× more.

It matters because it collapses three separately-tradeable quantities into one number, which is why Lawson's criteria can be plotted on a single axis and machines of wildly different design compared directly.

The record that has stood since 1995#

Machine Year Triple product
ZETA 1957 9.0 × 10¹⁴
TFTR 1995 7.9 × 10²⁰ ← highest ever achieved
JET — Joint European Torus (DTE3) 2023 1.4 × 10²⁰
SPARC 2027 (projected) 6.2 × 10²¹
ITER 2039 (projected) 7.4 × 10²¹

No tokamak has beaten TFTR's 1995 value in thirty-one years. Not a physics plateau — no machine capable of exceeding it was built between TFTR's shutdown in 1997 and the 2020s. See Appendix: Quantitative Progress Data for the full dataset and The Discourse for why this reframes the "thirty years away" complaint.

Beware: a temperature record without a confinement time is not a triple product. That conflation produced the ZETA retraction of 1958.
