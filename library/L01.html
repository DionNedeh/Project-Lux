What this reference supports

The researchers trained a neural dynamics model on 311 TCV pulses, including five high-performance examples. They used reinforcement learning to design ramp-down trajectories and tested those trajectories in experiments.

Where the evidence stops

The result concerns a specific phase of a discharge on one research tokamak. It does not demonstrate full-plant autonomy or a measured electricity saving.

Source & provenance

Wang, A. M. et al. Learning plasma dynamics and robust rampdown trajectories with predict-first experiments at TCV. Nature Communications 16, 8877 (2025).
