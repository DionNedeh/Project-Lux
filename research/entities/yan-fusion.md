Shanghai company co-founded by Stonehill Technology and fusion specialists. It is pursuing two superconducting [[stellarator]] generations: a hybrid low- and high-temperature-superconductor device followed by a fully HTS design.

The [[fia-global-fusion-industry-report-2026]] records **$293 million phased declared funding**, 40 employees and a planned 350 MWe commercial facility. It reports completion of China’s first production line for three-dimensional stellarator superconducting magnets, three-dimensional bending and winding of stainless-steel-armoured conductor, and fabrication and testing of n-class superconducting joints. Its planned intermediate device has a **1.8 m major radius**, a **3 T** field and hybrid LTS+HTS magnets.

YAN announces Q_sci > 1, engineering net energy and a first pilot plant in the 2030s. Those are projections. Its production base is in Hefei, home of [[asipp]], and Peking University is a named collaborator. The manufacturing claims are more mature evidence than the schedule, but remain company-reported.
