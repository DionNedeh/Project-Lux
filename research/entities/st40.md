[[tokamak-energy]]'s spherical tokamak in Oxfordshire. R ≈ 0.4 m, B up to ~3 T.

**Achieved 100 million K ion temperature in 2022** — the first privately funded device to do so, and remarkable for a machine that small. Its data appears in the Wurzel-Hsu Lawson dataset at a [[triple-product]] of 6.3 × 10¹⁸ (shot 10009, 2022).

Successor: **ST80-HTS**. Tokamak Energy was selected as **Magnet Systems Partner for [[step]]** (£70m) in 2026.
