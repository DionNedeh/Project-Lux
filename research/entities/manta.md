**M**odular, **A**djustable, **N**egative-**T**riangularity **[[arc|ARC]]** — a high-field compact fusion pilot plant design study, NASEM-compliant.

Its distinguishing choice is **[[negative-triangularity]]**, which gives good confinement **without an H-mode [[pedestal]] and therefore without [[elm|ELMs]]** — removing an entire failure mode by geometry rather than by control.

**Claimed overnight cost: $3.4 billion** — inside the NASEM threshold of $5 bn for a viable pilot plant. See [[lcoe-fusion]] for why design-study costs cluster at the optimistic end of a thirty-fold range.

Full design paper held locally: [[manta-negative-triangularity-pilot-plant]].
