Richmond, British Columbia. **Magnetized Target Fusion**: inject a magnetised plasma into a rotating cavity of liquid lead-lithium, then collapse the cavity mechanically.

The liquid metal is simultaneously **first wall, breeding blanket and coolant** — and being liquid, it **cannot be damaged by neutrons**. That is the architectural bet, and it sidesteps the field's hardest problem by construction.

## The first publicly listed fusion company

Completed its business combination with **Spring Valley Acquisition Corp. III on 10 July 2026**; trades on Nasdaq as **GFUZ**.

**The discrepancy is the story.** Institutional PIPE investors committed and were oversubscribed; the broader public market largely redeemed. Fusion's first genuine public-market price signal, and a cautious one. See [[34-investment-mechanics]].

**[[lm26]]** has reached 0.72 keV and is funded through end-2028 against three published [[milestone-tranching|milestones]].


**The 2025 near-failure.** In May 2025 General Fusion laid off **at least 25% of staff** while short
of cash — days after [[lm26]] successfully compressed plasma. It had raised **$440 m**; its previous
round was **$22.66 m**, closed July 2024. The SPAC merger fourteen months later makes this a
**near-failure and recovery**, not a death. Source: [[general-fusion-layoffs-2025]]; analysed
in [[45-survivorship-failure-and-retrenchment]].
