Xi’an developer of compact, repetitive-pulse HTS spherical tokamaks using magnetic-reconnection heating. The [[fia-global-fusion-industry-report-2026]] records 154 employees, a deuterium–tritium fuel plan and a lithium neutron blanket.

> [!warning] Unresolved funding conflict
> The FIA company profile gives **$138.9 million total declared funding**, while the report’s raise chart shows **$220 million raised during the survey year**. Both values are retained; neither is silently substituted for the other.

Startorus announced a 100 MWe first pilot plant for 2028. That is a company projection, not a demonstrated result, and the source profile supplies no achieved Q value or intermediate net-energy milestone. Its “Make Tokamaks Simple Again” framing rests on pulsed operation and magnetic-reconnection heating, which differentiates it from continuous conventional [[tokamak]] programmes but does not remove the blanket, tritium and high-duty-cycle engineering requirements. The conflicting funding figures remain listed in [[index]] as a known conflict.
