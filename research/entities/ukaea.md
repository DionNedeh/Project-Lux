The UK's fusion agency, at Culham. Operator of [[jet]] for forty years and now of its decommissioning.

## The programme

- **UK Fusion Energy Strategy, March 2026** — *"A new energy revolution"* — with **over £2.5 billion committed across five years**
- **[[step]]** — a prototype fusion power plant at West Burton, delivered through UK Industrial Fusion Solutions Ltd
- **[[librti]]** — £220 m, a global first-of-a-kind lithium breeding tritium facility
- **[[h3at]]** — tritium processing at plant scale, with Eni
- **[[race-culham]]** — remote handling and robotics
- **[[mast-upgrade]]** — the [[super-x-divertor]] testbed
- **[[jet]] Decommissioning and Repurposing** — running to ~2040
- **Culham AI Growth Zone** — £125 m, including £45 m for *Sunrise*, a 1.4 MW fusion-dedicated supercomputer

## Regulation

The **Energy Act 2023** formally **excluded fusion from nuclear site licensing**. Fusion facilities are regulated by the Environment Agency and HSE, not the ONR — making the UK the most permissive major jurisdiction, deliberately.

**12 August 2026: [[dennis-whyte]] appointed Chief Executive.**

## The Tritium Fuel Cycle division

Added 2026-10-19. UKAEA runs a dedicated **Tritium Fuel Cycle division**, which with [[librti]] is
developing the analytical techniques that [[tritium-accountancy|tritium accountancy]] lacks —
**HERA** for real-time gas-stream analysis and **THALIA** for tritium trapped in liquid breeders,
both at low technology readiness level ([[thalia-hera-ukaea-tritium-accountancy-2026]]).

This is the only live accountancy instrument-development programme in this vault, and it sits
alongside [[h3at]] in tritium handling.

## A third targeted bet: vanadium

Added 2026-11-02. UKAEA's manufacturing group at Rotherham is building a
**[[vanadium-alloys|V-4Cr-4Ti]] strategy** covering R&D, supply chain and industrialisation
([[ukaea-2026-vanadium-strategy]]) — the first source here to treat that supply chain at all. Its
long-term bets: a V44/liquid-lithium self-cooled blanket as *"the optimal solution for commercial
fusion powerplants"*, and a **domestic UK vanadium supply chain**. Qualification is being attempted
by a **novel digital route**, Bayesian optimisation with EPRI engaged for regulatory acceptance,
partnered with [[ornl|ORNL]], [[tokamak-energy|Tokamak Energy]] and ATI. **A strategy poster, not a
qualified material.**

## Assessment

Small in absolute terms, exceptionally well targeted. The UK has invested in **the three things
nobody else is adequately funding — tritium breeding, tritium handling, and now the structural
alloy that liquid-lithium breeding depends on** — plus a genuine prototype plant with construction
money and a site.
