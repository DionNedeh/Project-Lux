Operating Indian [[tokamak]] at the Institute for Plasma Research in Gandhinagar. It forms the domestic experimental pair with the superconducting [[sst-1|SST-1]] and supports the national plasma-physics and workforce base behind ITER-India.

The current Project Lux raw corpus names ADITYA-U and its operator but does not include a dedicated primary source for its machine parameters, upgrade history or campaign results. This page therefore records no unsourced dimensions, temperatures, pulse lengths or dates. It is medium-confidence pending a human-curated source.

Its structural significance is clear even from the limited evidence. India supplies major [[iter]] systems—including the cryostat, cryolines and heating equipment—and requires domestic machines on which scientists and engineers can develop diagnostics, control and operations experience. ADITYA-U is evidence of that continuing experimental capability; it is not, by itself, evidence of a national pilot-plant design or a demonstrated reactor-relevant Q value.
