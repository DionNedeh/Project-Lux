The trade association for private fusion, and publisher of the sector's survey of record.

Its **Global Fusion Industry Report** — sixth edition published **13 July 2026** — is the primary source for company-level funding, headcount, technical approach and timeline data throughout this wiki. See [[fia-global-fusion-industry-report-2026]].

**CEO Andrew Holland** writes unusually candid forewords. Two lines worth keeping:

> *"The last year has added an important new driver: the energy demand of artificial intelligence."*

> *"When we emerge into a fully commercial fusion industry, few expect there to be 56 companies in it. The race is on."*

The FIA also states plainly that its **Chinese coverage is incomplete** — several companies with reported government and private funding declined to respond — which means any national comparison drawn from its data understates Chinese activity.
