Munich. Laser-driven [[inertial-confinement]] using **nanostructured targets** with ultrashort pulses, and a proprietary fast-ignitor concept.

Building a **$150 million demonstration facility jointly with Colorado State University**, which leads the **RISE** hub of [[us-doe]]'s IFE-STAR programme.

Marvel's approach is one of the genuine attempts to **change the driver-energy scaling** rather than optimise within it — attacking the [[rayleigh-taylor-instability]] problem through target design rather than through driver symmetry.
