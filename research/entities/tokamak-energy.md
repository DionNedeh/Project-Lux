Oxfordshire. 300 staff. Spherical tokamak developer **and** a merchant magnet business through its **TE Magnetics** division.

- **[[st40]]** achieved **100 million K in 2022** — the first privately funded device to do so
- **Demo4** reached **11.8 T and 7 MA-turns** — the first full-scale tokamak-configuration HTS magnet demonstration (2026)
- **Selected as Magnet Systems Partner for the UK's [[step]] programme — £70 m / $95 m**

Successor device: **ST80-HTS**, which in turn informs the design of the **[[st-e1|ST-E1]]** power
plant — a 5 m, aspect-ratio-2.3, 5.25 T pre-concept design built around **phased operation**:
**300–500 MWe** net in a pilot phase, **800–1000 MWe** after a planned upgrade, with permanent
magnet cage and vessel separated from replaceable in-vessel systems
([[willis-2026-st-e1-pre-concept-design-overview]], an 11-paper Focus Collection). A [[us-doe]]
Milestone Program awardee.

Notable for breeding on **pure, unenriched lithium** — a deliberate route around the ⁶Li enrichment
chokepoint ([[lithium-6]]) — with [[vanadium-alloys|V-4Cr-4Ti]] structure in the centre-column
shield in place of [[rafm-steel|RAFM steel]]. Both choices were **evidenced on 2026-11-02** when the
Focus Collection's neutronics and fuel-cycle papers became reachable; the company also builds its
own neutronics and fuel-cycle codes, **G4Tokamak** and **PyFuel**
([[19-simulation-codes-nuclear-data]]).

> [!note] The company publishes against its own criteria, including where it falls short
> ST-E1's neutronics paper reports peak fast flux to the HTS magnets at about **2.4× its own
> stated limit**, giving ~13 full-power years against a 30 fpy intent
> ([[st-e1-neutronics-crossref-metadata-2026]], [[18-superconductors-and-magnets]]). That is a
> shortfall disclosed in a company-authored paper — worth recording as a point about this
> company's disclosure practice, not only about the design.

The magnet business matters beyond its revenue: alongside [[commonwealth-fusion-systems]] selling to [[realta-fusion]], it is evidence of a fusion **supply chain** forming rather than 56 vertically integrated prototypes. See [[20-fusion-beyond-electricity]].
