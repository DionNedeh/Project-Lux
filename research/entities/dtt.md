A fully Italian experimental device under construction at the ENEA Research Centre in Frascati — **the largest magnetic confinement facility under construction in Europe after [[iter]]**.

Part of the [[eurofusion]] programme, DTT exists to study **alternative solutions to the DEMO thermal load problem**: advanced magnetic configurations and [[divertor|divertors]] based on liquid metals.

## 2026 progress

- The **first 170 GHz / 1 MW [[gyrotron]]** for DTT's ECRH system passed acceptance testing at the **FALCON** facility in Lausanne: 1 MW for 100-second pulses, **95% reliability** (19 of 20 pulses), pre-series tube from THALES at 1.02 MW and 40% efficiency
- **July 2026:** power supplies for the divertor coils arrived at Frascati

The gyrotron procurement is a **joint F4E–DTT effort serving both ITER and DTT** — a rare instance of two major programmes sharing a component line.
