The Institute of Plasma Physics, Chinese Academy of Sciences, Hefei — the centre of China's magnetic fusion programme.

- **[[east]]** — the world long-pulse leader; 1,066 s steady-state H-mode
- **[[best-tokamak]]** — under construction; 582-tonne superconducting magnet tested August 2026
- **[[cfetr]]** — the planned engineering test reactor bridging ITER to DEMO
- **CRAFT** — a large fusion technology test complex for magnets, divertors and blankets, **built in parallel with the plasma machines rather than sequentially**

That last point is characteristic of the Chinese programme and is its main structural difference from Western practice.
