Goleta, California laser-fusion company founded by Shuji Nakamura, Richard Ogawa and Hiroaki Ohta. It proposes coherently combined fibre-laser amplifiers coupled to an optical enhancement cavity, aiming to raise laser efficiency and repetition rate for direct-drive [[inertial-confinement]].

The [[fia-global-fusion-industry-report-2026]] records **$37.5 million declared funding**, 18 employees, deuterium–tritium fuel and a lithium neutron blanket. The company reported more than **100,000× optical power enhancement** in its cavity, mirrors above **99.999% reflectivity**, and an eight-channel coherent beam-combination input system. These are laser-component demonstrations, not fusion-energy results.

Its announced schedule is Q_sci > 1 in 2033, Q_eng > 1 in 2034 and a 1,000 MWe first pilot plant in 2035. The gap between the demonstrated optical components and that integrated plant remains the target-production, chamber, blanket, duty-cycle and wall-plug-efficiency problem documented in [[05-inertial-confinement-fusion]].
