Israel. Compact fusion developer, and **the reactor partner in the Fusion Power Barge (FusPoB) project**.

FusPoB is a **Joint Industry Project** assessing the feasibility of installing and operating a compact fusion reactor aboard a vessel. The consortium is notable for who is in it: **nT-Tao**, **ABS** (a major ship classification society — the body that would have to certify it), **Siemens Energy**, **P&P Marine Consultants** and **TEMISTh**.

**The concept:** a containerised reactor generating up to **20 MWe** in a **71.4-metre barge**, driving **two 8,000 kWe steam generators**, with **battery backup giving six hours of autonomy at eight knots**. Commercial prototype targeted around **2032**.

**The presence of a classification society from the design stage is the significant detail** — the maritime equivalent of early regulator engagement.
