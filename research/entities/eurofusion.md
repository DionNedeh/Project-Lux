The pan-European fusion consortium — **30 members across 29 countries**, funded through **Euratom**.

Its *European Research Roadmap to the Realisation of Fusion Energy* has governed European fusion since 2012, targeting all know-how to **start [[eu-demo]] construction in the 2030s** and **fusion electricity on the European grid by 2050**.

## The 2026 shift

The roadmap is being substantially revised. EUROfusion states the programme *"will move from being laboratory-based and science-driven towards an industry- and technology-driven venture."*

- The **EU's first-ever Fusion Strategy** is due in 2026
- A **public-private partnership with the EU fusion industry** is being established
- Alignment to **ITER Baseline 2024** and Fusion Expert Group guidance

Coordinates access to [[wendelstein-7-x]], [[asdex-upgrade]], [[tcv]], WEST, [[dtt]] and [[mast-upgrade]].
