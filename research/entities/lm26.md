[[general-fusion]]'s **Magnetized Target Fusion** demonstrator: a magnetised plasma injected into a rotating cavity of liquid lead-lithium, then collapsed mechanically.

The liquid metal is simultaneously **first wall, breeding blanket and coolant** — and being liquid, it cannot suffer neutron damage. That is the architectural bet.

## Status

- Reached **0.72 keV** (~8.4 million °C) plasma heating
- ~$150m cash funds the programme **through end-2028**

## The milestone structure

LM26 is publicly tranched against **three technical milestones** — see [[milestone-tranching]]:

1. **1 keV**
2. **10 keV**
3. **Achieving the [[lawson-criterion|Lawson criterion]]**

Now that General Fusion trades publicly as GFUZ, progress against these is visible on a quarterly basis.


**Milestone against payroll.** LM26's plasma compression was followed within days by layoffs of at
least 25% of [[general-fusion]]'s staff — the clearest evidence in this corpus that a technical
milestone does not reliably produce capital. See [[45-survivorship-failure-and-retrenchment]].
