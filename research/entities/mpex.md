The **Material Plasma Exposure eXperiment** at [[ornl]] — a linear plasma device for exposing candidate plasma-facing materials to reactor-relevant plasma fluxes.

A [[us-doe]] roadmap **near-term** milestone: *"Commission MPEX to deliver plasma exposure testing and launch complementary domestic high-heat-flux testing."*

MPEX addresses the *plasma* side of plasma-material interaction; [[ifmif-dones]] and [[fpns]] address the *neutron* side. **Neither addresses both simultaneously**, which is what a real first wall experiences — and which the roadmap defers to the long term: *"validate tungsten-based and liquid-metal PFCs under fusion-prototypic neutron, heat, and plasma flux."*
