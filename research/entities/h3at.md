**Hydrogen-3 Advanced Technology** — a [[ukaea]] facility developed with **Eni** for **tritium processing and handling at plant scale**.

Its purpose is to develop the skills and technologies for processing and handling tritium within future fusion power plants — the practical counterpart to [[librti]]'s breeding work.

An oil major funding tritium infrastructure is itself a notable signal about how energy incumbents are positioning. See [[28-vacuum-wall-conditioning-tritium-plant]] for what a tritium plant actually contains.
