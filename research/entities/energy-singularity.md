Shanghai. Builder of **[[hh70]] — the world's first fully high-temperature-superconducting [[tokamak]]** — from a standing start in roughly **two years**.

- First plasma **June 2024**
- **1,337 seconds** of steady-state long-pulse operation
- **HH170**, targeting **Q ≥ 10**, construction due to complete **end-2027**
- Seeking $500 m

The construction speed is the point. It is the clearest single datum on Chinese industrial throughput in fusion, and it should be read alongside the observation that the FIA's coverage of Chinese private companies is **incomplete** — several with reported government and private funding declined to respond.
