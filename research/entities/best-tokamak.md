The **Burning plasma Experimental Superconducting Tokamak**, under construction at [[asipp]] in Hefei. Intended to demonstrate a [[burning-plasma]] and, per Chinese statements, fusion power generation on a schedule aggressive relative to [[iter]].

**4 August 2026: China completed testing of a 582-tonne superconducting magnet for BEST** — the largest single fusion magnet component tested to date.

BEST sits between [[east]] and [[cfetr]] in China's roadmap. Its supporting technology complex, **CRAFT**, is being built in parallel rather than sequentially — a characteristic feature of the Chinese programme.
