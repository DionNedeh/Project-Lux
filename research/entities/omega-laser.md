60 beams, 30 kJ, at the University of Rochester's Laboratory for Laser Energetics. Far smaller than [[nif]], but the world's leading facility for **[[direct-and-indirect-drive|direct drive]]** — the more efficient coupling scheme and the only one with plausible power-plant economics.

OMEGA has produced the best hydrodynamic scaling results in direct drive, and scaled to NIF energies its data suggests direct drive could reach ignition-relevant conditions.

LLE leads one of the three **IFE-STAR** hubs and signed a **$6.9m research agreement with [[focused-energy]]** in March 2026.
