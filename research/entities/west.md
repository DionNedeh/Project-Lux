**Tungsten (W) Environment in Steady-state Tokamak**, rebuilt from Tore Supra at CEA Cadarache.

An **actively cooled [[tungsten-pfc|tungsten]] divertor** with superconducting coils, designed specifically to test **ITER-like plasma-facing components under long pulses**.

WEST holds long-duration records in the tens of minutes for plasma with an actively cooled tungsten wall — a different and complementary record to [[east]]'s H-mode duration.

It is also where **high heat flux testing of newly developed tungsten components** is carried out in an actual tokamak rather than a test stand — see [[38-component-manufacturing]] — and one of the two machines where **thermal breakdown of diborane at 700 °C** was validated for [[iter]]'s [[boronization]] system.
