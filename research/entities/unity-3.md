**The first facility generating 14 MeV neutrons to evaluate commercial breeding blanket designs and validate tritium production.**

Built at [[ornl]] with **[[kyoto-fusioneering]]**, which relocated its US headquarters to Oak Ridge on **11 August 2026** to do it. Complements **UNITY-1** (Japan) and **UNITY-2** (Canada, under development).

## Why it matters more than its budget suggests

[[tritium-breeding-ratio]] is the field's second-hardest problem and has **never been demonstrated**. [[iter]]'s Test Blanket Modules are the first in-device test — **from 2039**.

And per [[nuclear-data-fendl]], a blanket design validated only computationally has not been validated: cross-section uncertainty is a meaningful fraction of the entire 10% design margin between TBR = 1.1 and TBR = 1.0.

UNITY-3, alongside the UK's [[librti]], is where that question gets answered this decade rather than next.
