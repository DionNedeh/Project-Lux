[[thea-energy|Thea Energy]]'s **3 × 3 planar [[rebco]] shaping-coil test array**, built to test the magnet-manufacturing and field-control approach intended for [[eos|Eos]]. It is a magnet prototype, not a plasma device.

The programme manufactured **nine coils from 45 double-pancake windings**, with conductor from three suppliers, and operated the integrated array at **20 K**. Two static field patterns measured 25 cm from the array matched predictions with RMS errors of **0.56% and 0.60%**, inside the programme's ±1% target. The winding line reached roughly half a day takt time and no more than three days total production time per double pancake.

The boundary matters. Canis was flat, whereas a reactor's shaping coils would conform to a three-dimensional surface. It generated no net toroidal flux, closed flux surfaces, quasisymmetry or plasma. Transient control, several quench cases, robustness to manufacturing defects and dense field-shaping-unit integration remained planned work. [[prototyping-test-canis-hts-planar-coil-array]] therefore establishes a component-level proof of concept, not an integrated stellarator result.

The gap it has to cross is now specified: [[helios|Helios]]'s design calls for **324** shaping coils plus 12 plasma-encircling coils at up to **20 T** on-coil ([[overview-of-the-helios-design]]). Canis was nine coils, flat, at 20 K.
