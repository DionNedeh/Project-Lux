**The world's first fully high-temperature-superconducting [[tokamak]]**, built by [[energy-singularity]] in Shanghai — **in roughly two years**.

- First plasma **June 2024**
- **1,337 seconds** of steady-state long-pulse operation

The construction speed is the headline. A private Chinese company built a complete fully-[[rebco]] tokamak from a standing start in about two years, at a declared total funding of $112.5m.

**Successor: HH170**, targeting **Q ≥ 10**, with construction due to complete **end-2027**.
