Garching and Greifswald. The deepest fusion research base in Europe.

- **[[wendelstein-7-x]]** (Greifswald) — the world's leading [[stellarator]], holder of the long-pulse [[triple-product]] record
- **[[asdex-upgrade]]** (Garching) — direct descendant of the machine where **[[h-mode]] was discovered in 1982**, and the source of much of what is known about [[tungsten-pfc|tungsten]] plasma-facing components, ELM control and [[detachment]]
- **GENE** — the gyrokinetic code used worldwide
- Stellarator optimisation theory underpinning [[quasi-symmetry]]

**[[proxima-fusion]]** spun out of IPP in 2023 and has since raised $752 m.
