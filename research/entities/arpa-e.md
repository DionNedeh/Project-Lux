The US Advanced Research Projects Agency – Energy. In fusion its role is targeted, high-risk intervention rather than programme funding.

- **SCALEUP: $20 million to [[thea-energy]]** (9 July 2026) to establish **the first US production line for modular [[rebco]] HTS magnets** — a direct attack on the sector's tightest physical bottleneck
- **CHADWICK** — fusion materials programme
- **LENR programme** — $10 million across eight projects (February 2023), explicitly framed as an attempt to *"break the stalemate"* and prove or debunk low-energy nuclear reactions. See [[21-the-graveyard]]

ARPA-E also funds [[shine-technologies]] and appears across the sector's collaboration lists.
