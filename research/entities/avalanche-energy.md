Seattle. Developing **modular 100 kWe to 5 MWe compact fusion machines** based on the **Orbitron** magneto-electrostatic confinement scheme.

**Deliberately tiny.** Anticipated commercial unit: **0.1 MWe**. The pitch is *"dual-use, mobile and distributed power applications across air, land, sea, and space"* — islanded microgrids in austere or remote environments, EV recharging, spacecraft power and propulsion.

That is defence procurement language, and the partner list matches: **DARPA, DIU, AFWERX** and Washington State. See [[37-military-dual-use-security]].

## Notable practice

- Publishes in **Nature**, **Physics of Plasmas** and IEEE — unusual for an electrostatic-confinement company, and a credibility signal
- Reported **stable plasma formation, pure hydrogen plasma, and 1 keV plasma temperature** in the past year
- Building **FusionWERX**, a commercial fusion test facility with tritium handling and superconducting magnets for neutron test stands
- Spin-out products already: high-voltage feedthroughs, alphavoltaics, high-entropy alloys

By not attempting grid scale, Avalanche sidesteps the scaling result that limits electrostatic confinement — see [[21-the-graveyard]].
