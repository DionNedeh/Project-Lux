Everett, Washington. **Sheared-flow-stabilised [[z-pinch]]** — a plasma column carrying its own current, stabilised against sausage and kink modes by an axial velocity shear, with **no external magnetic coils at all**.

Devices: **FuZE**, **FuZE-Q**, **Century**.

- Electron temperatures above **1 keV** (>10 million °C) demonstrated
- **#16 on TIME's Top GreenTech Companies 2026** — the highest-ranked fusion company
- [[us-doe]] Milestone Program awardee

If sheared-flow stabilisation holds at reactor-relevant currents, Zap's machine would be by far the **cheapest and simplest fusion device ever built**. That is a large "if" against a concept with a seventy-year history of instability — which is exactly what makes it worth watching. See [[21-the-graveyard]].
