**Appointed Chief Executive of the [[ukaea]] on 12 August 2026** — the most significant personnel move in fusion that year.

Formerly director of **MIT's Plasma Science and Fusion Center**, central to the [[sparc]]/[[arc]] design lineage and the high-field HTS thesis, and co-founder of the intellectual programme that produced [[commonwealth-fusion-systems]].

Moving to run the UK's fusion agency — which is delivering **[[step]]**, a *spherical* tokamak prototype power plant — places the leading advocate of compact high-field conventional tokamaks at the head of a national programme built on a different concept.

Whichever way that tension resolves, it is a substantial transatlantic transfer of expertise, and it says something about where the UK sees its programme going.

**Why individuals matter here:** in a field of ~5,600 direct employees, with a handful of people who have actually built a large fusion device, institutional knowledge is concentrated in individuals to an unusual degree. See [[27-people-institutions-public-trust]].
