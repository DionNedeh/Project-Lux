The Experimental Advanced Superconducting Tokamak at [[asipp]], Hefei. **The world's long-pulse leader.**

EAST's mission is **duration, not power**. It runs deuterium only and produces negligible fusion energy; it exists to answer whether a plasma can be held in [[h-mode]] for the timescales a power plant needs.

## Records

- **1,066 seconds of steady-state H-mode** (January 2025) — beating its own 403 s from 2023, and crossing the symbolically important 1,000-second mark
- 1,056 s of long-pulse plasma (2021)

## The January 2026 result

EAST reported experimental confirmation of a **"density-free region"** — evidence for a route past the [[greenwald-density-limit]], providing physical grounds for high-density operation of magnetic confinement devices.

If it generalises, it relaxes one of the tokamak's fundamental operational constraints, and it matters directly for reactor design because reactors want high density. As of August 2026 it is a single-machine result awaiting replication.

EAST's H-mode work feeds directly into [[iter]] and China's own [[best-tokamak]] and CFETR.
