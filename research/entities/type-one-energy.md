**[[infinity-two]]**: a **350 MW** plant at a **Tennessee Valley Authority** former coal site, targeted mid-2030s.

## Why it is credible

Published a **complete physics basis in the *Journal of Plasma Physics*, including breeder blanket and [[tritium-breeding-ratio|tritium fuel-cycle]] feasibility** — a level of public design detail rare for a private company, and one of the clearest signals separating serious efforts from pitch decks.

**6 August 2026:** licensed **four cryogenic [[pellet-injection|pellet fuelling]] inventions from [[ornl]]**, including an extrusion system producing pellets at **reactor-relevant rates**.

A [[us-doe]] Milestone Program awardee. Total funding past $160 m including an $87 m convertible note.
