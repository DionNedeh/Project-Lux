The Mega Ampere Spherical Tokamak Upgrade at [[ukaea]] Culham — **the world's leading testbed for the [[super-x-divertor]]**.

Its 2025 *Nature Energy* result — *first demonstration of Super-X divertor exhaust control for transient heat load management in compact fusion reactors* — is one of the more consequential exhaust results of the decade, precisely because the [[divertor]] problem is most acute in the compact designs the private sector is building.

MAST-U also underpins the UK's **[[step]]** programme, which as a spherical tokamak has both a severe exhaust problem and very little space in which to solve it.
