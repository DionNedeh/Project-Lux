Fontaine-based company combining a [[stellarator]] design with large-scale high-temperature-superconductor manufacture. The [[fia-global-fusion-industry-report-2026]] records **$65.68 million declared funding** and 120 employees as of July 2026.

Renaissance Fusion is unusual because the magnet-production method is both a reactor technology and a possible supply-chain business. It reported completing an HTS pilot line and its first deposition tests, demonstrating a stellarator magnetic field with a laser-engraved copper magnet at liquid-nitrogen temperature, and integrating Nicola100 HTS magnets with a **700 °C flowing-liquid-metal loop**. These are component demonstrations, not a fusion-energy result.

The company names CEA, Bpifrance, ESRF and Eindhoven University of Technology as collaborators. Its raw-source profile does not give a pilot-plant date, a power rating or a demonstrated Q value. The page therefore records no such projection. Its relevance is the intersection of [[rebco]] manufacture, liquid-metal engineering and the [[38-component-manufacturing|factory problem]] rather than a near-term plasma milestone.
