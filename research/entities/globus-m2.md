Operating spherical [[tokamak]] at the Ioffe Institute in St Petersburg. It appears in the Wurzel–Hsu Lawson dataset for 2019 and in the Russian machine inventories in [[04-magnetic-confinement-devices]] and [[08-national-programs]].

Its most consequential recent role in this corpus is operational rather than record-setting. A 2025 *Physics of Plasmas* paper named in [[15-references]] reports **inter-shot boronization** on Globus-M2: carborane is injected between discharges, using comparatively simple vacuum hardware, to maintain a low-impurity wall without stopping for a conventional glow-discharge campaign. The reported method outperformed conventional boronization and enabled longer experimental sessions.

That is a demonstrated machine result, not a general guarantee. It gives [[boronization]] a real-device precedent while leaving scale-up to machines such as [[iter]]—with kilometre-scale gas lines, many injection points and tritium-plant interfaces—as a separate engineering problem.
