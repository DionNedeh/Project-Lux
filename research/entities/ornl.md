The US national laboratory most central to fusion **technology** as distinct from plasma physics.

- **US ITER** management — delivered the [[iter]] central solenoid, completing final deliveries in March 2026, and in the process built US capability in large superconducting magnets that now serves the private sector directly
- **[[pellet-injection]]** — the world centre for four decades. [[wendelstein-7-x]]'s record discharge was fed by an ORNL injector; **four cryogenic fuelling inventions licensed to [[type-one-energy]] on 6 August 2026**
- **[[mpex]]** — Material Plasma Exposure eXperiment, for plasma-facing component testing
- **[[unity-3]]** — with [[kyoto-fusioneering]], the first facility generating 14 MeV neutrons to evaluate commercial breeding blanket designs
- Materials, blankets and tritium science

Kyoto Fusioneering relocated its US headquarters to Oak Ridge in August 2026 to build UNITY-3 alongside the laboratory.
