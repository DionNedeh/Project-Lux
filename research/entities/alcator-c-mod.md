MIT's high-field, compact tokamak — **the direct intellectual ancestor of [[sparc]]**.

Set the record for **plasma pressure in a magnetically confined plasma: 2.05 atmospheres**, on its final day of operation in September 2016.

C-Mod's entire research programme was an argument that **[[b4-scaling]] means high field beats large size**. [[commonwealth-fusion-systems]] was founded to prove it. Its 1160930033 shot (2016) reached a [[triple-product]] of 7.4 × 10¹⁹ at a density of 5.5 × 10²⁰ m⁻³ — extraordinary density for its size.
