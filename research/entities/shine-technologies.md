The outlier in the billion-dollar tier, and instructive because of it. Janesville, Wisconsin.

SHINE pursues fusion through a **staged commercial ladder**: industrial neutron imaging first, then **medical isotope production** (notably molybdenum-99), then nuclear waste recycling, and only then fusion energy.

**It has actual revenue today** from operating neutron sources — the only company in the billion-dollar tier making money now.

## Mo-99

**NNSA selected SHINE for $32 million** to complete its Mo-99 production facility, **approximately 75% complete**. A proposed Medicare rule includes a **$10 add-on payment for radiopharmaceuticals derived from domestically produced Mo-99 from 1 January 2026** — a direct policy subsidy for this supply chain.

Collaborators include ARPA-E, GE-Hitachi and the Department of Defense.

Its model — **sell neutrons before you sell electricity** — is now being copied across the sector. See [[20-fusion-beyond-electricity]].
