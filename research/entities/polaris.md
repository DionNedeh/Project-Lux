[[helion-energy]]'s prototype at Everett, Washington — **the first privately funded fusion machine to operate with deuterium-tritium fuel**.

- Reached **150 million °C** (announced February 2026)
- Running **nearly every day**, validating the electricity-recovery approach and refining technology for the commercial Orion plant

Polaris is not the machine that will sell power to Microsoft. That is **Orion**, under construction at Malaga, Washington, targeting **≥50 MW after a one-year ramp, in 2028**.
