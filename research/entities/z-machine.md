The largest pulsed-power facility in the world. Roughly **20 megaamperes** delivered to the load in about 100 nanoseconds, from around 20 MJ of stored energy.

Z drives **MagLIF** — Magnetized Liner Inertial Fusion — in which the current implodes a metal liner onto pre-magnetised, laser-preheated D-T fuel. It appears as a distinct experimental line in the Wurzel-Hsu Lawson dataset.

It also generates the most intense laboratory X-ray source on Earth, used for **Z-pinch-driven hohlraums** and, like [[nif]], for **stockpile stewardship** — which is what funds it.

Its architecture — Marx generators, water-dielectric pulse-forming lines, magnetically insulated transmission lines — is being superseded for commercial purposes by **Linear Transformer Drivers**, which are more compact, higher repetition rate and modular. See [[32-heavy-ion-fusion-and-pulsed-power]] and [[pacific-fusion]].
