The **Steady State Superconducting Tokamak** operated by India’s Institute for Plasma Research at Gandhinagar. It is the superconducting machine in India’s domestic pair, alongside the copper-coil [[aditya-u|ADITYA-U]].

Project Lux’s current raw corpus establishes its operator, operating status and steady-state superconducting role, but does not contain an ingested primary source for dimensions, field, current, pulse-length records or campaign dates. Those values are therefore not reproduced here. The page remains medium-confidence until such a source is human-curated into `raw/`.

SST-1 matters institutionally as much as physically. India’s domestic machines sustain the experimental and training base behind ITER-India, which supplies the [[iter]] cryostat, cryolines and heating systems. The machine is consequently part of the workforce and systems-integration pipeline described in [[27-people-institutions-public-trust]], not evidence that India has yet demonstrated a reactor-scale steady-state plasma.
