**The only operational experience anywhere of maintaining a tritium-contaminated tokamak**, and consequently the most valuable single body of experience in the field.

**No human has entered [[jet]]'s vacuum vessel in over thirty years.**

## The architecture

**Two articulated booms, each 12 metres long**, snake into the vacuum vessel through a port, carrying cameras, lighting, tools and end-effectors — supporting their own weight cantilevered from a single anchor point.

The primary end-effector is **MASCOT**: a **master-slave manipulator with haptic force feedback**. The operator moves a master arm in the control room; the slave reproduces the motion, and forces encountered are transmitted back to the operator's hands.

**That feedback is the essential feature.** The operator feels the weight of a component being carried, the resistance of a bolt, the moment a fastener seats. Without it, remote assembly of precision components is effectively impossible.

## The benchmark

JET's **ITER-Like Wall** installation — replacing the entire plasma-facing first wall — required **18 months of work, two shifts a day**, performed entirely remotely.

That is the benchmark for a first-wall replacement on a machine of JET's size. **A commercial plant must do something comparable on a routine schedule** — see [[availability]], where this single constraint can move LCOE by a factor of two.
