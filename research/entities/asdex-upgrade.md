Medium-sized, **all-[[tungsten-pfc|tungsten]] wall**, at [[max-planck-ipp]] Garching — and the direct descendant of the machine where **[[h-mode]] was discovered in 1982** by Fritz Wagner.

AUG remains one of the most scientifically productive tokamaks in the world. Much of the current understanding of:

- **Tungsten plasma-facing components** and their impurity behaviour
- **[[elm]] control by resonant magnetic perturbations**
- **[[detachment]] physics**
- **Flux pumping** and the sawtooth-free hybrid scenario

comes from it. Its [[shattered-pellet-injection]] and [[neoclassical-tearing-mode]] work is also widely used, and it is the reference machine for chemical trapping of diborane in [[boronization]].

Its 2016 shot 32305 appears in the Wurzel-Hsu dataset at a [[triple-product]] of 2.2 × 10¹⁹.
