The **Mirror Fusion Test Facility** at [[llnl]] — the culmination of a decade of US [[magnetic-mirror]] research, and fusion's canonical cautionary tale.

- Cost **$372 million** — at the time, **the most expensive project in LLNL history**
- **Construction completed in 1986**
- Operational budget cancelled the same year, amid Reagan-era energy research cuts
- **Formally cancelled in 1987 and disassembled without ever operating**

A $372 million machine was completed and then dismantled **without a single plasma shot**. The physics was never tested. US mirror research effectively ended for thirty years — until [[wham]] achieved 17 T in July 2024 and [[realta-fusion]] began commercialising it.

**The lesson:** a fusion programme's schedule risk includes the political cycle. A machine taking twelve years to build must survive three or four changes of government. See [[21-the-graveyard]].
