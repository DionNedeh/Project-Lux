Kyoto. **Notable for not building a reactor at all.**

Kyoto Fusioneering sells **[[gyrotron|gyrotrons]], tritium systems and blanket technology** to the rest of the industry — the clearest example of a fusion company positioned as a supplier rather than a developer.

Building the **UNITY** series of test facilities: **UNITY-1** (Japan), **UNITY-2** (Canada, under development), and now **[[unity-3]]** with [[ornl]].

**11 August 2026: relocated its US headquarters to Oak Ridge, Tennessee** to build UNITY-3 with the national laboratory — the first facility generating 14 MeV neutrons to evaluate commercial breeding blanket designs.

Its existence is one of the strongest structural signals in the sector: **an industry is a supply chain, not 56 vertically integrated prototypes.**
