The **Neutral Beam Test Facility** (PRIMA) at Consorzio RFX, Padua — built because [[iter]]'s beams were considered too risky to construct without a full-scale prototype.

- **SPIDER** — *Source for the Production of Ions of Deuterium Extracted from Rf plasma*. A full ITER-scale **negative ion source**, operating since **2018**. Develops caesium injection strategies, beam uniformity and co-extracted electron control
- **MITICA** — the complete **1 MV** injector: source, electrostatic accelerator, neutraliser, residual ion dump, calorimeter. In advanced construction

ITER's system: **33 MW**, ~1 MeV, **40 A of negative deuterium ions per injector**, ~16.5 MW delivered, **for one hour**.

**The existence of this facility is itself informative:** a *heating system* for ITER required a research programme comparable in scale to a mid-sized tokamak. Any company claiming a compact machine with substantial NBI power inherits that problem. See [[neutral-beam-injection]].
