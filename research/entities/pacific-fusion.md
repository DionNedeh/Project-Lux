The fastest-funded newcomer in the sector's history, founded by a team including former Meta CTO Mike Schroepfer and physicists from the [[nif]] and [[z-machine]] lineages.

Uses **coordinated electromagnetic pulses rather than lasers** to drive inertial confinement. Pulsed power converts wall-plug electricity to load energy at **over 50% efficiency**, versus a laser's ~10% aspiration — the strategic case for the whole category. See [[32-heavy-ion-fusion-and-pulsed-power]].

## Milestone-gated funding

Pacific Fusion's distinguishing practice is a **$1 billion Series A with milestone-based tranches** — capital committed but released only on demonstrated technical milestones, published in advance. See [[milestone-tranching]].

## Demonstrated

- **June 2026:** second milestone set achieved — a scaled module prototype delivering **more than 440 GW of peak output power at approximately 1.1 MV** in ultra-fast pulses
- **20 July 2026:** with [[llnl]], **surpassed 3,000 successful Sirius pulsed-power prototype tests**

That second item is the more informative one. **3,000 successful shots on a prototype module is a reliability statistic** — and reliability at high shot count is exactly what a pulsed plant needs and what no laser system has demonstrated.


**State sovereign capital.** The New Mexico State Investment Council, a state fund built on oil and
gas revenue, was reported in March 2026 to be backing a **$1 bn** Pacific Fusion project near
Albuquerque — a resource-revenue fund diversifying into the technology that would displace its own
revenue base. Single-outlet trade reporting. See [[44-state-capital-and-the-public-balance-sheet]].
