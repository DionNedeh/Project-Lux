The world's second-largest superconducting [[stellarator]] (heliotron configuration, R = 3.9 m, B = 3 T) and **the first large fusion device built with superconducting coils**.

**Operations concluded on 25 December 2025**, after 27 years and **200,000 plasma shots**. In its final campaign, 40% of experimental proposals came from outside Japan.

LHD's legacy is a vast steady-state and high-density stellarator database — ion temperatures above 100 million K, long-duration discharges, and much of what is known about impurity transport and divertor behaviour in helical devices. Its 2008 high-triple-product shot reached 5.2 × 10¹⁹.
