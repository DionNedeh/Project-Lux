The **Spherical Tokamak for Energy Production** — the UK's flagship, and a **prototype fusion power plant** rather than an experiment. Targets net electricity around **2040**, delivered by UK Industrial Fusion Solutions Ltd.

Sited at **West Burton, Nottinghamshire**, a former coal-fired power station.

## 2026 progress

- **First land parcel transferred from EDF on 23 March 2026 — three and a half years ahead of schedule**
- **£200 million** Construction Partner contract to the **ILIOS consortium** (Kier–NUVIA JV)
- **[[tokamak-energy]] selected as Magnet Systems Partner (£70m / $95m)**
- Site capacity tripled to ~120 professionals
- First public consultation ran 14 January – 11 March 2026
- **August 2026: Dennis Whyte appointed CEO of [[ukaea]]** — the MIT PSFC director and [[sparc]]/[[arc]] co-architect now leads the agency delivering a spherical tokamak

Its distinguishing bet is that the [[spherical-tokamak|spherical configuration]]'s high
[[plasma-beta|beta]] compensates for lower achievable field. Its distinguishing risk is the centre
column, which must carry conductor and shielding in almost no space — a risk
[[st-e1-neutronics-crossref-metadata-2026|ST-E1's neutronics]] quantifies for a sibling design at
**≈ 50 dpa per full-power year** and a magnet life well short of intent.

## Fuel cycle, modelled externally

[[meschini-2023-tritium-fuel-cycle-arc-step|Meschini *et al.* 2023]] model **two STEP-class
variants** — one with a liquid-lithium blanket, one with an encapsulated breeding blanket of FLiBe
pebbles, gas-cooled — and find required [[tritium-breeding-ratio|TBR]] below 1.2 achievable only
with [[tritium-burn-fraction|burn efficiency]] of 0.5–1%, [[availability|availability]] above 70%,
processing under 4 h and [[direct-internal-recycling|DIR]]. **An independent academic model of a
STEP-*class* machine, not UKAEA's own fuel-cycle analysis**, which this vault does not hold.

[[ukaea-2026-vanadium-strategy|UKAEA's vanadium strategy]] names the spherical tokamak with a
liquid-metal breeder blanket as its target asset, with [[vanadium-alloys|V-4Cr-4Ti]] qualification
as the enabling work.
