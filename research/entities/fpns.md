The US counterpart to [[ifmif-dones]] — a fusion-spectrum neutron source for materials qualification.

Placed in the **mid-term (3–5 year)** construction window of the [[us-doe]] Fusion Science and Technology Roadmap, i.e. **not operating before the early 2030s**.

The roadmap's sequencing is explicit: near-term (2–3 yr) neutron-source surrogates and non-nuclear blanket test stands; **mid-term construct FPNS**; long-term (5–10 yr) integrate with private-sector pilot plant programmes.

**Read that against the private sector's schedules.** Companies plan pilot plants for the early-to-mid 2030s. The facility that would qualify their materials begins producing meaningful data in roughly the same period.
