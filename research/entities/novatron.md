Stockholm developer of an open [[magnetic-mirror]] concept using a proposed ponderomotive double trap to improve axial confinement of ions and electrons.

The [[fia-global-fusion-industry-report-2026]] records **$34.45 million declared funding**, 80 employees and more than **1,000 plasma experiments on Novatron 1** in the preceding year. It names an N2 proof-of-concept device and an N3 pilot plant as intermediate steps, with KTH, UKAEA, Studsvik, VTT, St1, InnoEnergy, Freemelt and Fusion for Energy among the collaborators.

Its announced schedule is Q_sci > 1 in 2030, engineering net energy and a first pilot plant in 2035, with 1.5 MWe stated commercial output. These are company projections, not results. Novatron belongs beside [[realta-fusion]] and [[wham]] in the mirror revival, but its confinement claim is a distinct technical route and should not be treated as validation of the broader mirror category.
