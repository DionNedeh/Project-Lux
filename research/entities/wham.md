The **Wisconsin HTS Axisymmetric Mirror** at UW-Madison — the machine that revived the [[magnetic-mirror]] after thirty years.

## Two records

- **15 July 2024: 17 tesla on a confined plasma** — the highest magnetic field ever applied to a fusion plasma
- **19 June 2026: [[direct-energy-conversion]] demonstrated** — [[realta-fusion]] became the first commercial fusion company to convert plasma energy directly to electricity

What changed since [[mftf-b]] was cancelled in 1986 is not the physics of end losses. It is **[[rebco]]**, which allows mirror ratios unattainable in the 1980s — and a higher mirror ratio directly shrinks the velocity-space loss cone that killed the concept.
