Home of **[[nif]]**, where [[ignition]] was achieved on 5 December 2022 and has been repeated eleven times.

Also home of **[[mftf-b]]** — the $372 m [[magnetic-mirror]] completed in 1986 and dismantled in 1987 without ever operating. Both the field's greatest success and its canonical cautionary tale, in one institution.

## Current work

- NIF ignition campaign; **8.6 MJ record, target gain 4.13** (7 April 2025)
- Leads the **STARFIRE** hub of DOE's IFE-STAR programme
- **With [[inertia-enterprises]]**: cryogenic D-T fuel layering cut from days to 2–3 hours (August 2026)
- **With [[pacific-fusion]]**: 3,000+ successful Sirius pulsed-power prototype tests (July 2026)
- 2026 *Nature* and *Nature Physics* work resolving a 20% discrepancy in diamond melting at extreme pressure — simulations suggest **fusion gain could triple** if other degradation mechanisms are controlled

NIF's primary mission remains **stockpile stewardship**. See [[37-military-dual-use-security]].
