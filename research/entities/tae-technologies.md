The oldest company in the modern sector and the longest-running private fusion effort anywhere. Foothill Ranch, California.

Beam-driven [[field-reversed-configuration]]: [[neutral-beam-injection|neutral beams]] both sustain the FRC against decay and heat it. Machine lineage **C-2 → C-2U → C-2W ("Norm") → Copernicus → Da Vinci**.

## 2025-26

TAE announced that **Norm outperformed expectations**, achieving FRC formation using neutral beams alone — a substantial simplification — and that it would **skip the planned Copernicus device** and move directly toward Da Vinci, its first commercial plant.

> [!warning] Unresolved conflict
> Reporting since has been inconsistent about whether Copernicus is cancelled or merely deferred. Sources conflict; this is not resolved here.

## Revenue businesses

Unusually, TAE has built real revenue around spin-offs — **TAE Power Solutions** (power management and energy storage) and **TAE Life Sciences** (boron neutron capture therapy). A materially different capital strategy from the rest of the sector.

**5 August 2026:** strategic **helium-3 supply partnership with [[black-moon-energy]]**.

## The honest gap

[[aneutronic-fusion|p-¹¹B]] needs roughly 3 billion K. Norm has reached ~75 million K for ~30 ms. TAE does not dispute this; its argument is that an FRC's β ≈ 1 eventually makes the fuel accessible where no other geometry can.
