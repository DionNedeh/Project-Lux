The case for the ICF driver class that was **cancelled in 2011/12** — and, on the evidence, the most credible dormant concept in fusion.

Heavy ion accelerators solve, **by construction**, the three engineering problems that make laser IFE hard:

| Power plant needs | Laser | Accelerator |
|---|---|---|
| Wall-plug efficiency ~10% | ~0.5% ([[nif]]) | **25–40%, today** |
| Repetition rate 10–20 Hz | ~1 shot per hours | **Native** |
| Final optics survival | Degrade; unsolved | **Magnets stand off and are shielded** |

Ions also deposit energy by **classical stopping power** — no laser-plasma instability, no hot-electron preheat.

**It was cancelled because nobody had demonstrated ignition.** The National Ignition Campaign then failed in September 2012; [[nif]] ignited in December 2022.

**The concept was killed for lacking a proof that now exists.** No major programme has restarted it. See [[32-heavy-ion-fusion-and-pulsed-power]].
