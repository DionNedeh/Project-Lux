A high-field compact fusion pilot plant design study: **M**odular, **A**djustable, **N**egative-**T**riangularity **[[arc|ARC]]**.

Its distinguishing choice is **[[negative-triangularity]]** — reversing the plasma cross-section curvature to obtain good confinement **without an H-mode [[pedestal]] and therefore without [[elm|ELMs]]**, removing an entire failure mode by geometry rather than by control.

**Claimed overnight cost: $3.4 billion**, inside the NASEM threshold of $5 bn for a viable pilot plant. See [[lcoe-fusion]] for why design-study costs cluster at the optimistic end of a thirty-fold range.
