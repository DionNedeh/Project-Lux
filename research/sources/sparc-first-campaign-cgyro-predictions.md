First-principles gyrokinetic prediction of [[sparc]]'s first-campaign performance, using nonlinear **CGYRO**.

Its significance is methodological as much as physical: **the performance basis for a multi-billion-dollar machine, and for the [[us-doe]] roadmap's national "Milestone 1", is a turbulence simulation.** If the transport model is wrong, the machine underperforms and there is no cheap way to find out first.

SPARC's design goal is **Q > 2**; the nominal projection is **Q ≈ 11** at H98,y2 = 1. The gap between those two numbers is the uncertainty in extrapolating confinement scaling into a regime nobody has operated. See [[31-plasma-physics-deeper-cuts]].
