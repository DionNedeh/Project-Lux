One of the more consequential exhaust results of the decade, precisely because the [[divertor]] problem is most acute in the compact high-field designs the private sector is building — the scrape-off layer narrows as machines get larger and fields stronger.

Published in *Nature Energy* (2025). Underpins the exhaust strategy for the UK's [[step]].
