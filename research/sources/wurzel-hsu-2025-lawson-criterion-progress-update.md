**The field's canonical harmonised dataset**, and the source for every [[triple-product]] and Q_sci figure in this wiki. Updates the authors' *Phys. Plasmas* 29, 062103 (2022) paper.

Its value is that it **applies consistent definitions across machines and decades**, correcting for the fact that laboratories have historically defined confinement time, peak versus volume-averaged quantities, and gain in incompatible ways.

- **Table I** — tokamaks and spherical tokamaks
- **Table II** — other MCF and lower-density MIF
- **Table III** — ICF and higher-density MIF
- **Table IV** — experiments achieving appreciable Q_sci

## Two findings that reframe the field

**1. The tokamak [[triple-product]] record has stood since 1995.** [[tftr]] shot 83546, 17 February 1995: **7.9 × 10²⁰ keV·m⁻³·s**. [[jet]]'s 2021 and 2023 D-T campaigns came in *below* it. Projected: [[sparc]] 6.2 × 10²¹, [[iter]] 7.4 × 10²¹ — roughly 8–9× the best ever achieved.

**2. JET's famous "Q = 0.67" is a transient peak.** The harmonised value for shot 42976 is **0.27** (P_F = 1.1 × 10⁷ W against P_in = 4.0 × 10⁷ W). Both are correct; they measure different things. See [[q-gain-definitions]].

The authors also note it should be **"Lawson criteria", plural** — see [[lawson-criterion]].
