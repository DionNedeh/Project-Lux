## Approach split (56 companies)

Magnetic confinement 26 · inertial 12 · magneto-inertial 9 · hybrid electrostatic 7 · muon-catalysed 1.
**Stellarators (8) now outnumber tokamaks (5).** Fuel: D-T 40, p-¹¹B 5, D-D 5, D-³He 2.

## The challenge ranking (near-term / long-term)

Funding 30/18 · power efficiency and high Q 29/23 · **neutron-resilient materials 29/25** · **tritium self-sufficiency 25/23** · integrated systems engineering 19/10 · **plasma science 15/7** · supply chain readiness 15/20.

**The industry ranks plasma science seventh.** Materials and tritium sit at the top and stay there.

## Stated caveat

The FIA states plainly that its **Chinese coverage is incomplete** — several companies with reported government and private funding declined to respond. Any national comparison drawn from this data understates Chinese activity.
