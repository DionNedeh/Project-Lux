# First Light Fusion Announces Strategic Update

The company's own announcement, March 2025, that it would **stop trying to build a fusion power
plant**. It is the corpus's clearest documented case of a well-funded fusion company abandoning
energy production as a goal, and it anchors [[45-survivorship-failure-and-retrenchment]].

## What it states

- [[first-light-fusion]] **will not continue** its plans to develop a fusion power plant.
- **Machine 4** — its proposed ignition demonstration plant, developed with [[ukaea]] and
  Tractebel — is **discontinued**.
- The company will instead supply its **amplifier** technology to the wider inertial fusion
  industry, and pursue non-fusion R&D applications in space and defence.
- Claimed amplifier performance: **more than double** the pressure and **75%** greater speed on
  existing gas guns.
- The new model is described as **"capital-light, high-margin"**, generating revenue from that year.
- A maiden partnership with NASA and the Open University is announced.

## How to read it

**This is a company statement about its own retrenchment, and reads accordingly.** Per
`CLAUDE.md` §7.3 the amplifier performance figures are *announced*, not demonstrated: they are
uncorroborated by any independent measurement in this corpus. The framing — "strategic update",
"capital-light, high-margin" — is the company's own, and describes a company narrowing its ambition
after raising roughly $140 m and failing to find a route to a plant.

The substance is not in doubt, however, and it is unusually clear evidence: the abandonment of
Machine 4 is a definite, dated, self-reported fact, and it removes a named
[[05-inertial-confinement-fusion|inertial confinement]] power-plant programme from the field.

**What the source does not say** is why. It gives no account of the technical or financial reasoning
behind dropping Machine 4, and no independent valuation. Trade coverage at the time reported a
substantially reduced valuation; that reporting is not captured here and is not relied upon.
