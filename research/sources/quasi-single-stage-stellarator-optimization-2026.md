A method advance in [[stellarator]] design: **quasi-single-stage (QSS) optimisation folds coil feasibility directly into plasma-boundary optimisation** rather than solving the two problems sequentially.

QSS-optimised configurations exhibit **smoother plasma boundaries and winding surfaces, lower normal-field reconstruction errors, and reduced coil complexity**, while preserving favourable magnetic-symmetry and transport properties.

This matters commercially, not just academically. [[wendelstein-7-x]]'s 50 non-planar coils at ~1 mm tolerance are the reason stellarators are expensive to build. **Every reduction in coil complexity is a direct reduction in the cost of the concept** — which is the same problem [[thea-energy]] attacks from the other direction with planar coil arrays.
