The argument that fusion's commercialisation depends on social and environmental questions that the technical literature largely ignores.

Related consequential life-cycle assessment work finds, for a hypothetical ITER-style plant:

- **Low global warming potential** compared with other energy technologies — one tokamak LCA found fusion emits **less CO₂ than photovoltaic solar and less than half that of fission**
- **Higher water usage**, driven by **mining and refining of critical minerals like [[lithium-6|lithium]]**
- Social impacts concentrated in **critical materials supply chains**

Impact categories deliberately include **water usage, toxicity of material inputs, supply chain stability and worker hours** — the last two rarely appearing in conventional LCAs.

**The honest position:** fusion's operational footprint is excellent; its *upstream* footprint is a normal industrial materials footprint, and it is not zero. See [[33-safety-environment-decommissioning]].
