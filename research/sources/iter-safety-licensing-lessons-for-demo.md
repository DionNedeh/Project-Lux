The reference document for **how the fusion safety case was actually made**.

[[iter]] was licensed as a French **Installation Nucléaire de Base** in November 2012 — **the first fusion device in the world licensed as a nuclear installation**. Its safety file is consequently the template every subsequent regulator consults, including for [[eu-demo]] and for private developers.

## The key framing

The safety objective — adopted by every design since — is that **no accident should require evacuation of the public beyond the site boundary**. Far more demanding than "fusion is safe", and achievable because the mobilisable inventory is bounded and there is no energy source capable of dispersing it far.

Fusion safety is a **confinement problem, not a reactivity problem**: no criticality, no decay-heat meltdown, no chain reaction — but kilograms of tritium and tonnes of activated dust. See [[33-safety-environment-decommissioning]].
