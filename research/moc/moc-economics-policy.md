## The numbers that matter

- **[[lcoe-fusion]]** — $1,400–43,000/kW FOAK spread; mature LCOE $110–145/MWh, and the independent analysis calls even that optimistic
- **[[availability]]** — inversely proportional to LCOE, and never demonstrated
- **[[recirculating-power]]** — why Q_eng > 1 needs Q_sci of 10–30+
- **[[milestone-tranching]]** — the sector's most important financial innovation
