## The four things to know

1. **[[ignition]] was achieved** in December 2022 and repeated eleven times. The seventy-year physics threshold is crossed
2. **No magnetic device has produced net energy.** [[sparc]] attempts it in **2027**
3. **The binding constraints are now [[helium-embrittlement|materials]] and [[tritium-breeding-ratio|tritium]]**, not plasma physics — the industry's own survey ranks plasma science seventh
4. **The tokamak [[triple-product]] record has stood since 1995** — a procurement stall, not a physics stall
