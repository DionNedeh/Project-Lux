A plasma heated predominantly by its own fusion products rather than by external systems. Conventionally **Q ≳ 5**, where alpha heating equals external heating; ITER's Q = 10 puts alphas at roughly two-thirds of total heating.

**No magnetic device has ever produced one.** This is the specific gap [[iter]] exists to close, and it is why the machine is as large as it is.

## Why it is qualitatively different

Three new problem classes appear at once:

1. **Fast-alpha confinement** — 3.5 MeV alphas have large orbits. Field ripple, resonances or 3D error fields lose them before they thermalise, taking the heating with them and depositing energy on the wall.
2. **[[alfven-eigenmodes]]** — a super-Alfvénic alpha population resonantly drives modes that then transport the alphas out. Self-defeating, and unresolved at reactor scale.
3. **[[burn-control]]** — fusion power depends on temperature, temperature depends on alpha heating. A coupled nonlinear system with positive feedback that nobody has operated.

[[nif]] has produced burning plasmas, but for ~100 picoseconds, which does not test any of the above.
