Compress the fuel with a nanosecond pulse, then ignite it separately with a picosecond **petawatt** pulse — or with a laser-accelerated ion beam.

Because compression no longer has to create the hot spot, the **symmetry requirement relaxes** and gain could rise substantially. That is the entire appeal: it attacks the [[rayleigh-taylor-instability]] problem by removing the need for extreme convergence.

## The difficulty

Coupling the ignitor energy into a compressed core requires penetrating a dense plasma corona — usually via a re-entrant cone, or by ion-beam standoff. Demonstrated at small scale; **not at ignition scale**.

**Pursued by:** [[focused-energy]] (proton fast ignition) and [[marvel-fusion]] (nanostructured targets with ultrashort pulses).
