The [[lawson-criterion|Lawson]] criteria reformulated for [[inertial-confinement]], where confinement time is set by disassembly rather than transport.

## Thresholds

- **ρR ≥ 0.3 g/cm²** in the hot spot — the stopping range of a 3.5 MeV alpha in D-T at 10 keV. Below it, alphas escape and cannot bootstrap the burn. This is the [[ignition]] condition
- **ρR ≈ 1–3 g/cm²** total fuel — required for meaningful burn fraction

## Burn fraction

```
f ≈ ρR / (ρR + H_B)     with  H_B ≈ 7 g/cm²
```

At ρR = 3 g/cm², that is about **30%**. Most of the fuel is never burned.

This is why ICF fuel efficiency is intrinsically low, and why gain must come from compressing a large mass rather than burning all of it — which in turn drives the driver energy requirement, and therefore the entire cost structure of an inertial fusion plant.
