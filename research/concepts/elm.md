The [[pedestal]] does not sit gently against its stability limit. On reaching the peeling-ballooning boundary it **collapses**, expelling several percent of the plasma's stored energy onto the [[divertor]] in **under a millisecond**.

Uncontrolled Type-I ELMs would destroy a reactor divertor. Mitigation is mandatory, not optional.

## Escape routes, in rough order of maturity

- **Resonant magnetic perturbations** — 3D fields that degrade the pedestal slightly and suppress ELMs entirely. Works; costs confinement
- **Pellet pacing** — deliberately trigger small ELMs before large ones build
- **Quasi-continuous exhaust (QCE)** — a naturally small-ELM regime. **Demonstrated in D-T on [[jet]]'s DTE3**
- **X-point radiator (XPR)** — a stable strongly radiating region near the X-point. **Also demonstrated in D-T on JET**
- **[[negative-triangularity]]** — good confinement with no H-mode pedestal at all, and therefore no ELMs
- **I-mode, EDA H-mode, QH-mode** — naturally ELM-free regimes of varying reactor relevance

That JET demonstrated two of these in actual D-T plasmas in its final campaign is, in retrospect, among the most valuable results it produced.
