An empirical operational ceiling on tokamak density:

```
n_G [10²⁰ m⁻³] = I_p [MA] / (π a² [m²])
```

Exceeding it has historically triggered [[disruption|disruptions]]. Since reactors want **high** density — fusion power goes as n² — this is a direct constraint on achievable power density.

## The 2026 development

In January 2026 **[[east]]** reported experimental confirmation of a **"density-free region"** — evidence for a route past the limit, providing physical grounds for high-density operation of magnetic confinement devices.

If it generalises, it relaxes one of the tokamak's fundamental operational constraints. As of August 2026 it is a single-machine result awaiting replication.

[[arc]] deliberately operates at a conservative Greenwald fraction as part of its margin-to-limits design philosophy.
