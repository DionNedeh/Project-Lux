The isotope on which the entire D-T fuel cycle depends, via `n + ⁶Li → T + ⁴He + 4.8 MeV`.

## The bottleneck

Natural lithium is **7.5% ⁶Li**. Breeding blankets need **30–90% enrichment**.

> [!warning] Not every blanket does — and that changes the chokepoint argument
> Added 2026-10-19. **[[st-e1|ST-E1]] breeds on pure, unenriched lithium.**
> [[willis-2026-st-e1-pre-concept-design-overview|Willis *et al.* 2026]] select it deliberately, to
> reach a high *volumetric* [[tritium-breeding-ratio|TBR]] *"without reliance on Li-6 enrichment"*
> and without extensive use of toxic multipliers such as [[beryllium|beryllium]], while keeping
> radioactive waste acceptable.
>
> The 30–90% figure above holds for the designs it describes, which are the majority. What changes
> is the inference: **enrichment is a property of particular blanket architectures, not of D-T
> fusion.** A low-aspect-ratio device with thick, high-coverage blanket volume can trade geometry
> for enrichment — so the exposure below threatens *most* current designs, not the fuel cycle.
>
> **Evidenced 2026-11-02**, superseding this page's earlier record that the claim was asserted and
> an attempt to evidence it had failed. [[st-e1-tritium-systems-crossref-metadata-2026|086004]],
> read in full, selects **natural liquid lithium** outright. No breeding blanket of any kind has
> operated in a device.

**Historic enrichment capacity is concentrated in Russia and China** — a sovereignty exposure flagged by the DOE roadmap and industry analysis, alongside [[rebco]] tape, tungsten and helium ([[22-critical-materials-supply-chains]]).

A second-order cost: life-cycle assessment finds fusion's **water usage higher** than comparable technologies, driven by mining and refining critical minerals ([[33-safety-environment-decommissioning]]).

The UK's **[[librti]]** (£220m, 2025/26–2029/30) is the first facility purpose-built for lithium breeding technology at scale.

> [!note] Enrichment is necessary but not sufficient
> Solving ⁶Li enrichment does not solve breeding. [[abdou-2021-dt-fuel-cycle-tritium-self-sufficiency|Abdou *et al.* 2021]]
> put the **maximum achievable [[tritium-breeding-ratio|TBR]] at 1.15** even with detailed blanket
> designs, and showed the requirement is set by plasma [[tritium-burn-fraction|burn fraction]] and
> fuel processing time rather than by breeder composition. A plant with abundant enriched lithium and
> a 0.36% burn fraction still fails. Enrichment is one of several necessary conditions, and not the
> binding one.

> [!note] One architecture routes around enrichment — evidenced 2026-11-02
> [[ukaea-2026-vanadium-strategy|UKAEA]] states that [[vanadium-alloys|V-4Cr-4Ti]]'s properties
> *"reduce the need for neutron multipliers or lithium-6 enrichment in liquid lithium breeder
> blanket systems"*, and [[st-e1-tritium-systems-crossref-metadata-2026|ST-E1]] selects **natural
> lithium** outright. The Russia/China concentration therefore binds a **class** of blanket
> designs, not D-T fusion — at the cost of the vanadium supply chain.
