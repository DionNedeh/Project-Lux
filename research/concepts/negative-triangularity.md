Reversing the curvature of the plasma cross-section so the outboard boundary is concave rather than convex.

The payoff: **good core confinement without an [[h-mode]] [[pedestal]]** — and therefore **no [[elm|ELMs]] at all**. It removes an entire failure mode by geometry rather than by control.

Explored most thoroughly on [[diii-d]] and [[tcv]]. It is the basis of the **[[manta]]** pilot plant design (*Modular, Adjustable, Negative-Triangularity [[arc|ARC]]*).

## The trade

Negative triangularity plasmas historically achieve lower beta limits and are harder to shape at reactor scale, and the confinement gain relative to L-mode does not fully match a good H-mode pedestal. The bet is that **avoiding ELMs and disruption exposure is worth more than peak performance** — the same design philosophy that leads [[arc]] to sit deliberately below every stability limit.
