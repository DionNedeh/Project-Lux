Why [[inertial-confinement]] is hard, and why the **National Ignition Campaign failed in 2012**.

A light fluid pushing a heavy one is unstable. In ICF this happens twice — at the ablation front during acceleration, and at the hot-spot interface during deceleration. At a convergence ratio of 25–40, **any asymmetry is amplified enormously**.

## The seeds are mundane

- **The capsule fill tube** — a few-micron hole in a perfect sphere, injecting a jet of ablator material into the hot spot
- **Surface roughness and voids**, growing by factors of hundreds
- **Hohlraum drive asymmetry** and laser power imbalance
- **Laser-plasma instabilities** — stimulated Raman and Brillouin scattering, two-plasmon decay — scattering light and pre-heating the fuel with hot electrons

A cold, high-Z spike penetrating the hot spot radiates its energy away and quenches the burn.

## The lesson

The one-dimensional physics published by Nuckolls in *Nature* in 1972 was essentially correct. **ICF's limiting variable is manufacturing precision, not physics.** The enabling change for the 8.6 MJ record of April 2025 was reported as *a higher-quality diamond capsule* — not any change to the laser.
