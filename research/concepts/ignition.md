The state in which alpha self-heating alone sustains the plasma against all losses — external heating can be switched off. Equivalent to **Q = ∞**.

**Achieved 5 December 2022** at [[nif]], shot N221204: **3.15 MJ from 2.05 MJ**, target gain 1.5. The first time in history a controlled fusion reaction released more energy than was delivered to it, and the first crossing of a threshold [[lawson-criterion|Lawson]] wrote down in 1955.

Repeated **eleven times** as of 20 June 2026. Best result: **8.6 MJ, gain 4.13** (7 April 2025).

## In ICF specifically

Hot-spot ignition requires [[areal-density]] ρR ≥ **0.3 g/cm²** — the stopping range of a 3.5 MeV alpha in D-T at 10 keV. Below it, alphas escape and cannot bootstrap the burn.

The **Christopherson criterion** separates hot-spot ignition from *propagating burn*: the transition occurs when alpha heating has amplified yield by **15–25×** over the compression-only case, corresponding to a fractional alpha energy f_α ≈ 1.4.

## What it is not

Ignition is not net energy. See [[q-gain-definitions]]. No magnetic device has ignited; [[sparc]] targets Q > 1, not ignition.
